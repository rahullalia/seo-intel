## 2026-10-05 | On rsla.io the blog took 95 percent of organic clicks in 90 days and booked no call

- Source: RSL/A measurement, rsla.io, measured 2026-10-05, Google Search Console (sc-domain:rsla.io, 2026-07-06 to 2026-10-03, grouped by page, fragment URLs left out) and GA4 property 516387648 (2026-07-07 to 2026-10-04, Organic Search sessions by landing page)
- Measured: Blog URLs took 560 of 587 organic clicks on about 194,000 impressions, a click rate near 0.29 percent. The seven live service URLs took 2 clicks on 690 impressions, and the home page 22 clicks on 1,846 impressions. In GA4, 545 organic sessions landing on the blog produced 5 key events, 10 landing on service pages produced 0, and 57 landing on the home and contact pages produced 2. All 7 organic key events were cta_click (6) or newsletter_subscribe (1). The one booking_confirmed in the window came from Direct.
- Means: The blog is where rsla.io gets seen, and across 90 days it did not book a call by itself. This is a snapshot, not a test of page types, because the service pages earned almost no impressions and the 13 rewritten money pages on branch brand-visual-system were not live in the window. Re-measure 90 days after they ship to get both sides of a change, which is what would lift this entry to grade C.
- Factor: purchase-intent-page-type | Evidence: D | Lever: conversion | Scope: organic | Stage: audit
