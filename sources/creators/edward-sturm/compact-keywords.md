## 1. The claim

Sturm sells one idea: target the searches of people ready to buy with a short page that offers the product, in place of articles written for people who want to learn.

- Quote: "Blog SEO = answering “how, what, why, etc” with SEO, then trying to convert those visitors."
- Quote: "Compact Keywords SEO = scalable SEO landing pages for high-intent scenarios."
- Source: Edward Sturm, "Compact Keywords: SEO That Makes More but Requires Less", 2026-10-05 (undated sales page, date read), https://edwardsturm.com/compact-keywords/

He treats it as a different axis from head against long-tail keywords.

- Quote: "The real trick to SEO isn’t head vs. long-tail keywords. It’s compact vs. non-compact. Purchase-intent vs. exploratory."
- Source: Edward Sturm, "SEO – Down the Funnel", 2024-04-14, https://edwardsturm.com/articles/seo-down-the-funnel/

His four reasons, all from the sales page: these searchers convert without persuasion, few sites target their wording so the pages need fewer words and fewer backlinks, Google updates have demoted long articles, and AI answers have replaced how, what and why searches. The one study he links is the Ahrefs AI Overviews study, filed at grade B in `sources/experts/signals.md` under 2025-04-17.

- Quote: "AI Overviews in Google reduce clickthrough rate for blog SEO by 34%"
- Source: Edward Sturm, "Compact Keywords: SEO That Makes More but Requires Less", 2026-10-05 (undated sales page, date read), https://edwardsturm.com/compact-keywords/

## 2. What counts as a compact keyword

The searcher knows the thing they want and has no brand in mind.

- Quote: "Purchase-intent. The searcher knows what they’re looking for, they just don’t know the brand that will give it to them."
- Source: Edward Sturm, "SEO – Down the Funnel", 2024-04-14, https://edwardsturm.com/articles/seo-down-the-funnel/

The intent is one of three actions: buy, use, or call.

- Quote: "keywords where people are ready to buy, to use something, or to make a discovery call – but these in-heat searchers don’t know a brand to give them what they want."
- Source: Edward Sturm, "I Search SEO Topics All Day – And I Keep Running Into Myself", 2026-05-11, https://edwardsturm.com/articles/video-seo-content-discoverability-system/

A question is not one.

- Quote: "is a question, not a solution. Don’t target questions."
- Source: Edward Sturm, "Pareto SEO: The 20% of SEO That Matters", 2026-03-13, https://edwardsturm.com/articles/pareto-seo/

His worked examples by funnel stage, from the earlier sales page. Software: top "What is collaboration software", middle "Enterprise collaboration software", bottom "Collaboration software with intuitive learning". Agency: top "Chatbot use cases", middle "AI consulting companies", bottom "Artificial intelligence & chatbot development company". Later examples: "voice note recorder for dentists" and "handmade artisan ceramic mugs" in 2026-02-16, and for a plumber "same day water heater replacement" against the article keyword "how does a water heater work" in 2026-02-02.

- Source: Edward Sturm, "OLD Compact Keywords", 2026-10-05 (undated earlier sales page, date read), https://edwardsturm.com/old-page-compact-keywords/
- Source: Edward Sturm, "How to Use Bing’s New AI Performance Report to Get More LLM Citations", 2026-02-16, https://edwardsturm.com/articles/bing-webmaster-tools-ai-performance-report-grounding-queries-get-more-llm-citations/
- Source: Edward Sturm, "How PageRank Decay Lets You Rank Buyer-Intent SEO Pages With Less Work", 2026-02-02, https://edwardsturm.com/articles/pagerank-decay-rank-buyer-intent-pages/

## 3. Keyword patterns he gives away

A buying keyword plus an audience. His modifiers include schools, districts, hospitals, government, teams, enterprise companies, small businesses, students and lawyers.

- Quote: "Bottom of funnel keyword + for + plural modifier."
- Source: Edward Sturm, "Easy SEO to Get Big Purchase Orders", 2025-03-23, https://edwardsturm.com/articles/easy-seo-big-purchase-orders/

A task plus the word services. His examples: business license filing services, IRS audit support services, corporate retreat planning services.

- Quote: "Rank for every task in your field + “services”"
- Source: Edward Sturm, "How to do SEO for services businesses in under a minute", 2025-05-18, https://edwardsturm.com/articles/seo-for-services-businesses-under-one-minute-read-time/

Price words that brands avoid, a tactic he credits to James Dooley.

- Quote: "Searchers, on the other hand, specifically search for and click on “cheap,” “inexpensive,” “affordable,” “low-cost,” “budget.”"
- Source: Edward Sturm, "2 Easy SEO Tricks: The “Cheap” Modifier and $50 Micro-Influencers", 2026-08-11, https://edwardsturm.com/articles/2-easy-seo-tricks-cheap-modifier-micro-influencers/

Best-of lists inside your own niche, kept few. His advice on them moves: make a few in December 2025, and in February 2026 he writes that they are being hit.

- Quote: "Don’t spam these “Best X for Y” listicles. They should be a fraction of your site’s content output."
- Source: Edward Sturm, "5 SEO Things That Will Make You More Money, Get You More Visibility, and Make You a Happier Person", 2025-12-07, https://edwardsturm.com/articles/5-seo-things-to-make-more-money-more-visibility-more-happiness/
- Quote: "Compact Keywords are not about making best X for Y listicles which are getting destroyed right now:"
- Source: Edward Sturm, "How to Use Bing’s New AI Performance Report to Get More LLM Citations", 2026-02-16, https://edwardsturm.com/articles/bing-webmaster-tools-ai-performance-report-grounding-queries-get-more-llm-citations/

Competitor names. He gives these as video titles.

- Quote: "Video title: [Competitor] vs [Your Brand]: Which Is Better for [Use Case]?"
- Quote: "Video title: [Competitor] Alternative: Best Option for [Specific Buyer Type]"
- Source: Edward Sturm, "Before They Buy Your Competitor, Make This Video", 2026-06-22, https://edwardsturm.com/articles/before-they-buy-your-competitor-make-this-video/

Your own brand plus a doubt.

- Quote: "targeting keywords like “[brand] review,” “does [brand] work,” “[brand] honest review,” “[brand] scam,” “is [brand] legit,” etc."
- Source: Edward Sturm, "The Testimonial Video I Almost Didn’t Post Drove My Best Sales Day Ever", 2026-10-04, https://edwardsturm.com/articles/testimonial-video-marketing/

- Quote: "^(who|what|where|when|why|how|was|did|do|is|are|aren’t|won’t|does|if)[” “]"

## 4. How he finds and qualifies them

His order of questions. The first is who the competitors are. After the two quoted below come which results pages are under-targeted, then a sort by monthly search volume.

- Quote: "The second question should be what are they ranking for and not targeting properly. The third question should be out of those keywords which will bring me money."
- Source: Edward Sturm, "How SEOs Actually Find Keywords in 2026 (What Reddit Gets Right and Wrong)", 2026-09-16, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yIsli0iMQQs, at 07:44 (auto captions)

The procedure: put a seed term into a keyword tool, find a ranking site that places the keyword in its page title and URL slug, read what else that site ranks for, and pick out the buying keywords. Then run the same pass over your own Search Console queries.

- Quote: "what you specifically want to look for is a site that has that keyword in the page title and the URL slug."
- Source: Edward Sturm, "How I Find Low-Competition Keywords That Actually Convert", 2026-02-23, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_EYvOGlR2dw, at 01:38 (auto captions)

The test for an easy keyword is the results page, not a tool score: few ranking pages carry the keyword in the title and the slug.

- Quote: "The biggest indicators though are that there is a page title and URL slug that is just not that doesn't have the keyword in it."
- Source: Edward Sturm, "How I Find Low-Competition Keywords That Actually Convert", 2026-02-23, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_EYvOGlR2dw, at 08:41 (auto captions)
- Quote: "Don't depend on keyword difficulty metrics from SEO tools."
- Source: Edward Sturm, "How SEOs Actually Find Keywords in 2026 (What Reddit Gets Right and Wrong)", 2026-09-16, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yIsli0iMQQs, at 06:04 (auto captions)

A new or weak site also wants one weak site already ranking.

- Quote: "If you can see one low domain authority in the SER and not a lot of pages targeting that keyword, it's usually pretty certain that if you just target the keyword properly"
- Source: Edward Sturm, "How I Find Low-Competition Keywords That Actually Convert", 2026-02-23, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_EYvOGlR2dw, at 06:31 (auto captions)

Low volume is expected, and he says tools understate it. His one data point is his own page for "voice recording for students", which the article sets against a Moz estimate of 11 to 50 clicks a month.

- Quote: "the mistake that most people make is they will ignore keywords that are low search volume but that's actually where the opportunity is"
- Source: Edward Sturm, "You’re Ignoring the Best SEO Keywords – Here’s What to Do Instead", 2025-02-15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Nyne5Ym4mao, at 07:10 (auto captions)
- Quote: "Actual monthly clicks from Google search (not paid) to this page: 356"
- Source: Edward Sturm, "More Traffic Than You Think", 2023-09-24, https://edwardsturm.com/articles/keyword-tool-estimates-are-innacurate-do-this-instead/

Check the page type that ranks. Products ranking means buyers, articles ranking means learners. In 2026 he adds a caution for a competitive keyword where articles outrank product pages.

- Quote: "If the entire first page of Google is just articles teaching you stuff, that means it’s more top-of-funnel."
- Source: Edward Sturm, "My Love Letter to Bottom-of-Funnel SEO", 2023-06-25, https://edwardsturm.com/articles/bottom-of-funnel-seo-love-letter/
- Quote: "then you know that for this keyword, people actually don't have the same high intent that you thought that they had."
- Source: Edward Sturm, "How SEOs Actually Find Keywords in 2026 (What Reddit Gets Right and Wrong)", 2026-09-16, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yIsli0iMQQs, at 04:58 (auto captions)

In Search Console, the queries worth a page or an edit.

- Quote: "High impressions + position 3-20 + low CTR + strong relevance."
- Source: Edward Sturm, "Google Search Console’s New AI Update Is a Goldmine for Easy SEO Wins", 2026-03-09, https://edwardsturm.com/articles/google-search-console-ai-seo-wins/

The course teaches the research in Moz at 49 dollars a month and accepts Ahrefs or Semrush, per the sales page FAQ.

## 5. The page

Length. He has said 300 to 500 words since 2023 and prints an average of 415 with no sample behind it. In 2026 he also says length is relative to what already ranks.

- Quote: "300-500 words per page (the average is 415 words)."
- Source: Edward Sturm, "My Love Letter to Bottom-of-Funnel SEO", 2023-06-25, https://edwardsturm.com/articles/bottom-of-funnel-seo-love-letter/
- Quote: "These are 400-500 word pages explaining why you are a great choice for this thing the searcher is looking to achieve right now."

- Source: Edward Sturm, "How I Find Low-Competition Keywords That Actually Convert", 2026-02-23, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_EYvOGlR2dw, at 07:36 (auto captions)

Where the keyword goes: page title, meta description, URL, H1 and the start of the first sentence. On 2026-09-14 he calls the meta description optional.

- Quote: "The following is 70% of on-page SEO."
- Source: Edward Sturm, "Pareto SEO: The 20% of SEO That Matters", 2026-03-13, https://edwardsturm.com/articles/pareto-seo/

- Source: Edward Sturm, "Four", 2025-02-02, https://edwardsturm.com/articles/four-useful-things-for-social-media-marketing-and-seo/

Title formula: the keyword, then a benefit, then the brand.

- Quote: "NYC Website Migration SEO Services | Migrate Without Losing Traffic | Sturm Media"
- Source: Edward Sturm, "Four", 2025-02-02, https://edwardsturm.com/articles/four-useful-things-for-social-media-marketing-and-seo/

Before writing, three questions: who is searching, what do they want, what are they trying to achieve.

- Quote: "First, spend 5-10 minutes thinking and writing through these 3 questions:"

The skeleton, from 2025: a clear H1, one relevant image, a short introduction, a button, copy about this buyer, a second button at the bottom.

- Quote: "3-5 sentences of introductory text about how your brand is great for this keyword."
- Quote: "Put thought into who is searching this, their specific industry, and their specific needs. Give lots of examples."
- Source: Edward Sturm, "Easy SEO to Get Big Purchase Orders", 2025-03-23, https://edwardsturm.com/articles/easy-seo-big-purchase-orders/

Above the fold.

- Quote: "Do your best to satisfy the intent of your target searcher before they have to start scrolling on your page."
- Source: Edward Sturm, "Pareto SEO: The 20% of SEO That Matters", 2026-03-13, https://edwardsturm.com/articles/pareto-seo/
- Quote: "The most important thing: right away, with as simple language as possible, tell visitors what your product/service is and the main benefit they get."

- Quote: "Use buyer-intent FAQs that answer the exact objections people have right before purchasing"

## 6. Site structure

One hub page lists every buying keyword and links to a page for each. The hub is linked from the footer and the pages sit under one subfolder such as /services/ or /uses/.

- Quote: "You put all your bottom-of-funnel keywords on one page. This page is called either Uses or Services."
- Source: Edward Sturm, "My Love Letter to Bottom-of-Funnel SEO", 2023-06-25, https://edwardsturm.com/articles/bottom-of-funnel-seo-love-letter/
- Quote: "“Services” is linked to in footer and goes to this hub page."
- Source: Edward Sturm, "How to do SEO for services businesses in under a minute", 2025-05-18, https://edwardsturm.com/articles/seo-for-services-businesses-under-one-minute-read-time/

The homepage is for conversion, not for a keyword.

- Quote: "I recommend optimizing your homepage purely for conversions."
- Quote: "give dedicated pages for these keywords and have these pages be one to two clicks away from the homepage"
- Source: Edward Sturm, "Stop Targeting Multiple Keywords on Your Homepage. It’s Costing You Money.", 2026-05-27, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GS--rNYK7hc, at 03:54 (auto captions)

More pages is not the goal.

- Quote: "You would MUCH rather have a few excellent SEO pages ranking #1 than many mediocre ones not ranking #1."
- Source: Edward Sturm, "Content Refreshes in SEO: How to Double Your Traffic Fast", 2025-09-01, https://edwardsturm.com/articles/content-refreshes-seo-double-traffic-fast/

On cannibalization he says a small site can ignore it, and that the fix is to improve the page that already ranks.

- Quote: "it's a lot better to instead improve the page that is actually ranking."
- Source: Edward Sturm, "Keyword Cannibalization: What It Is, When to Worry, and How to Fix It", 2025-07-14, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n2FYCgQQfIY, at 04:27 (auto captions)

## 7. After publishing

Submit the page in Search Console and wait. His stated timelines are page one within a month or two and position one in six to eight months. One of his own pages took 8 weeks to reach page one.

- Quote: "Compact Keywords often rank on the first page of Google within the first month or two."
- Source: Edward Sturm, "OLD Compact Keywords", 2026-10-05 (undated earlier sales page, date read), https://edwardsturm.com/old-page-compact-keywords/
- Quote: "it took 8 weeks after publishing to rank on the first page of Google. I didn’t drive any backlinks to the specific page."
- Source: Edward Sturm, "Using SEO to Get Guest Writers, Clout, and Users", 2024-08-11, https://edwardsturm.com/articles/get-guest-writers/

Then link to it from pages that already rank. He finds them by searching his own domain with the keyword in quotes.

- Quote: "If the first page that shows could satisfy search intent for your target keyword, link the most relevant text on the other pages to that first page."
- Source: Edward Sturm, "Rank Faster With the Topical Authority You Already Have", 2026-09-14, https://edwardsturm.com/articles/topical-authority-rank-faster/

Two fixes he reports from one case each: re-dating old articles, and cloning a page that will not rank to a new URL with a 301 from the old one.

- Quote: "Take your best-performing articles from the last couple of years and update the date to this year."
- Source: Edward Sturm, "Freshness", 2024-04-21, https://edwardsturm.com/articles/freshness/
- Quote: "I cloned the not-ranking page to a new page."
- Source: Edward Sturm, "10-Minute SEO Hack That Took My Page From Invisible to #1 on Google", 2025-05-04, https://edwardsturm.com/articles/10-minute-seo-hack-that-took-my-page-from-invisible-to-1-on-google/

## 8. Links and authority

Links go to the domain, not to each page.

- Quote: "Link building goes to the domain, not the pages. A rising tide lifts all boats."
- Source: Edward Sturm, "OLD Compact Keywords", 2026-10-05 (undated earlier sales page, date read), https://edwardsturm.com/old-page-compact-keywords/

For a competitive keyword he swaps backlinks for internal links: rank several easy pages, then point them at the hard one. He calls the pattern SEO colonies. The article titled as needing no backlinks still asks for a base of them first.

- Quote: "First, make sure you have at least foundational backlinks."
- Quote: "First, ranking for easy keywords, then, using those rankings to internal link to your page targeting your more competitive keyword."
- Quote: "For anchor text, sometimes use the exact match keyword you’re targeting, or a partial match, or a naked URL:"

## 10. Off-site and AI search

The same placement rule applies on every platform he does not own: video titles and descriptions, Reddit posts, LinkedIn articles, press releases.

- Quote: "The most important thing is having your target keyword at the beginning of all the fields you have control over."
