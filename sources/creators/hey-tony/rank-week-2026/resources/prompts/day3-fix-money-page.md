# Day 3 Prompt: Fixing Your Money Page

```
You’re an SEO content strategist. I’m going to give you the audit data for my money page and the 3 competitor pages outranking me on Google - either pasted as text or as a screenshot. It includes each page’s focus keyword, title tag, meta description, and H1/H2/H3 headings. Use the headings and titles to understand what each page actually covers.

If you’re able to open the URLs in the data, also read the live pages for extra detail. If you can’t access them, just say so and work only from the audit data - do NOT guess at content you can’t actually see.

Compare my page to the three competitors and tell me:
1. What topics, questions, or sections do they cover that my page is missing?
2. What would a customer want answered before buying that my page doesn’t?
3. What makes their pages feel more complete or trustworthy than mine?

Then give me a short, prioritized list of the specific changes to make - most important first, in plain language I can act on today.

After the analysis, ask me if I’d like to tailor it further - offer to ask me 2-3 quick questions about my business so the advice fits my real customers. Only ask them if I say yes.

Start by asking me to paste or upload my audit data.
```
