# Insiders Welcome Call — 22.06.2026 (Matt Diamante)

## Tactics & step-by-step how-to
- Access the tools dashboard at **app.htinsiders.com** — first-time login uses "Create your account" with the **exact same email** used to sign up for Insiders; if email trouble, contact support (see below).
- Inside the tools dashboard: add your website under settings first — several tools unlock "bonus features" once your site is added — and set your **search country** to your actual country.
- **AI Blog Post Writer**: type in the topic/question you want written about; it interviews you (~7-10 questions), then generates the post. Runs on **Claude**, written to follow Matt's own content rules. Positioned as a free alternative if you don't want to pay for ChatGPT.
- **Blog Idea Finder**: type in a niche (e.g. "Nail Salon") and it returns blog topic ideas/questions — same concept as findquestions.com but doesn't require entering an email to get results.
- **Money Page / Service Page Analyzer**: pick a service page on your site, optionally enter the keyword you want it to rank for (or leave blank and AI suggests one), and it analyzes your page against competitors — word count gap, and who's ranking #1/#3 for that term. **Before acting on any recommendation, check where you're actually currently ranking** — if you're already ranking well (e.g. #2), don't touch the page just because the tool flags a gap vs. the #1/#3 pages. Can rerun a page anytime; capped at 20 audits/pages per month.
- **Link/Backlink Gap Analyzer**: shows backlinks your competitors have that your site doesn't — flagged as backlinks you could realistically go acquire.
- **Rank Report Card**: run your full site through an audit — checks page speed, crawlability, heading-tag structure, alt text, meta description length — returns a score out of 100 plus a pass/fail breakdown per factor.
- **Topic Cluster Analyzer**: feed it your site/sitemap; it scans all your blog content and shows what topics you're actually clustering around (vs. what you think you're writing about) — pillar/cluster view.
- **Cannibalized Keywords Scanner**: auto-pulls your sitemap and scans your pages to flag when more than one page is targeting the same keyword/topic (each page should target one main keyword).
- Categories/tags on a resource-library-style site: if tags/categories are structurally required for on-site search/filtering but the resulting tag/category pages are thin (no title tag, meta description, or H1), **no-index those pages** rather than removing the functionality; consider removing the excess tags later.
- Use the platform's built-in **AI help/search button** (separate from the top search bar) to ask full natural-language questions (e.g. "what's the best way to do keyword research?") — it pulls answers from the full call/transcript/post archive and cites the specific source videos. The plain top search bar only does literal keyword matching and struggles with multi-word/phrase queries.
- Call replays include searchable transcripts and a "chapters"-style jump feature — search any word (e.g. "ranking") to jump to every place it's mentioned across the whole call archive.
- When impressions rise but clicks/ranking stay flat: don't panic — treat it as a sign of ranking progress further down the funnel (more keywords ranking, just not page-1-top yet), keep producing content, and expect informational-content clicks broadly to get squeezed by AI Overviews regardless of your own execution.
- Route specific technical/diagnostic questions to the right specialist call rather than DMing Matt: Friday = new-insiders Q&A/share-your-site call (hosted by Matt); Thursday = technical SEO call (Denroy + Elliot); Wednesday = web design for SEO call (Leanne Jones).

## Tradeoffs & nuance
- Tags aren't universally banned — they're wrong for most typical business blogs (orphaned, content-less pages) but legitimately necessary for structures like a searchable resource library; the fix in that case is no-index, not removal.
- Tool-suggested competitor benchmarks (word count, "should rank #1/#3") are directional, not prescriptive — they must be filtered through your actual current ranking position before acting, or you risk breaking a page that's already performing.
- Rising impressions with flat/lagging clicks is reframed as progress rather than failure, but Matt is explicit this reassurance is partly circumstantial: the new Google feature that shows AI Overview appearance counts isn't available to most clients yet ("I have one client who has it. The rest of them don't, which is super annoying."), so the funnel-progress read can't currently be fully verified with data for most site owners.
- The platform's two search mechanisms trade off differently: the literal top search bar is precise but brittle on multi-word intent; the AI help button handles natural questions better but is limited to what's already been said in the platform's own content archive.
