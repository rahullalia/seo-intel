Schema markup is a file that most people will never see. And if you're a user, if you're visiting a website, you'll never know notice it, you'll never know that they have it or don't. But essentially, schema markup is a file that gives search engines a little bit more information about whatever your website has going on. So schema markup is used to provide information to search engines in order to understand your content. So this could be your hours of operation, your address, your logo, your, the description of your business, your product description, your product pricing, you know, how many reviews your products have, or what's the average review of your product, you know, all that kind of stuff. And it also helps them identify. And when I say them, I mean search engines. It helps search engines identify what type of page you have. So is it a service page, is it a blog post, is it an article, is it a product page? All that kind of stuff.
