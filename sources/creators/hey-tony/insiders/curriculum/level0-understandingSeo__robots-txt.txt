The robots. Txt or Robots Txt file is a hidden file that's hosted on your website. It's not really hidden, it's just users don't access it, but search engines do. So it's used primarily to manage Google's crawler traffic to your website and usually keep a file or page off of Google. So if you don't want somebody to find a file, a specific, you know, image or specific PDF or something like that or or specific page on your website, you can tell Google like, hey, don't look at this page and they'll ignore that page. You can also use your Robots text file to tell Google not to show orphan pages or not to index a page users need to be logged in to see. So stuff like that.
