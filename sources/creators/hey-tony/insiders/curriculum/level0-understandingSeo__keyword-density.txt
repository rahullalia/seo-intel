How many times are you using your focus keyword on a specific page of your website? This is called keyword density. So it's the amount of times you're using that keyword in relation to the total amount of words on that page. So, for example, if you're using the keyword 10 times on a page that has a thousand words, your keyword density will be 1%, which is 1% of all the words on the page are that keyword. You don't want your keyword density to be too high. This is, there's no steadfast rule for what percentage of keyword density you should have because some people, you know, they'll have like a 10% keyword density, which is crazy high. But if they're ranking number one for that, they're doing something right on that page. So it's going to be on a case by case basis. But you should try to aim, you know, to use your keyword a good amount of times on your page. If you only mention, you know, you say you have a, a page about blueberry pancakes and you only talk about, and you only use the keyword blueberry pancakes once on that page, you're not really sending a lot of signals to search engines or to people that that page is about blueberry pancakes. Because if it was about blueberry pancakes, you'd probably say like, our blueberry pancakes are made with, you know, whatever ingredients and then like the best ways to enjoy blueberry pancakes or the best temperature to cook blueberry pancakes at so the blueberries don't get like watery or burnt or whatever it is. So just in that example alone, you can see on that page, we would use the keyword three or four or five times. And that's without even going. The reason why keyword density is a thing is because back in the day, people used to do something which was called keyword stuffing, where they would just have the keyword like over and over again at the bottom of their page. So that search engines are like, oh, this page is about blueberry pancakes, even though they just said blueberry pancakes 500 times underneath it. So keep an eye on your keyword density. It's basically how many times you're using that keyword in relation to the total amount of words on the page.
