## 1. The rules Matt repeats most (the short list)

- **Answer your customers' real questions, consistently, on your own website.** "The people ranking right now are the ones answering their customers' questions. Consistently. On their own website. That's it. That's the whole thing." (2026-05-09) Source of questions: the last 10 questions in your inbox ("That's your content calendar for the next 2 and a half months"), Findquestions.com, AnswerThePublic.
- **Information gain.** "Because of all the AI slop out there, Google is looking for new, unique content." Never prompt "write me a blog post about TOPIC." Have ChatGPT interview you instead so the content carries "Your views, opinions, personal stories, failures, etc." (2026-05-14) Be human: "the best way to get into the AI results is to be HUMAN" (2026-06-04).
- **Keyword placement, the 90% rule.** "The most common reason a page doesn't rank is because it doesn't include the keyword they want to rank for.. and even if it does, it's usually mentioned once!" Put the keyword in: title tag, meta description, H1, at least one H2, the content at least twice, the URL if you can. "If you do that, you'll be better off than 90% of websites out there!" (2025-06-19)
- **Money page = what someone lands on ready to buy.** "services, service + city combos, industries served, product or pricing pages." Content page = "blog posts, guides, FAQs, comparisons. Anything someone lands on while researching." Junk to ignore: "tag pages, author pages, pagination, /cart/, /privacy-policy/, media attachments." (2026-08-04)
- **Benchmark, do not guess.** Audit your page against the #1 and #2 results for your exact keyword on 12 fields (title, meta, H1, H2s, H3s, word count, keyword usage, density, speed), then set your word count to the competitors' range and your density similar to theirs. Never keyword-stuff. (2025-07-05, 2025-12-08, 2025-12-09)
- **Internal links are "magic."** 3 links out of every new post + 3 links into it from older pages; 3 incoming links per money page; 5 to 10 strategic links per sweep. "Internal linking = SEO momentum." (2025-12-07, 2025-12-10, 2025-12-04)
- **Consistency beats everything.** One post a week, every week. "SEO is just consistent tiny wins stacked over time." Content "compounds"; consistency "matters more than talent or tactics." (2025-12-07, 2026-02-06)
- **Rank on Google and AI follows.** "it covers ranking on AI (hint, it's the same process as ranking on Google)." (2025-11-06) "It's not about schema markup or advanced strategies." (2026-06-18)
- **Work in the right order.** The missing piece is "a clear, step-by-step process for knowing exactly what to work on, and doing it in the right order", not more content, tools, or budget. Outputs, in order: keyword-optimized pages, intent-built content, internal links "the way Google wants to see them", a plan for what is next. (2026-02-18)
- **Get SEO right while the site is being built.** "It's a lot easier to get SEO right from the start than to fix it later." (2026-03-06)

## 2. Page-building tips, by page type

Matt's emails are light on page layout (the calls and his book carry that; see `page-types-master.md`). What the emails do pin down:

### Homepage
- Counter-example, not a recipe: "The site is one page, 35 words, no meta description, no h2 tags... and it's completely vibe coded" and "it's ranking #1 for a ton of keywords." The why was behind a link. Treat as a data point that niche intent beats polish, not as permission to build 35-word pages. (2026-08-11)
- Your sitemap lives at homepage URL + `/sitemap.xml`; it is the raw material for page planning and internal linking. (2026-08-04, 2025-12-10)

### Service pages and money pages
- Definition and scope: "the product or service page that should be making you the most money", a page "you actually want people to buy from or contact you through." (2025-12-08)
- Which money pages to build next: run the Topic Gap Analysis (section 3) against a competitor's sitemap; rank gaps "by revenue potential, not search volume. A page that's one step from a phone call beats a page that gets traffic and nothing else." A competitor cluster of "4+ pages on one theme" is a bigger signal than one missing page. (2026-08-04)
- Audience and copy stance: write the page "for a customer who's comparing two or three local companies right now, not someone doing research." (2026-08-04)
- On-page checklist for a money page, his exact five: title tag, meta description, headings, content improvements, internal links. (2025-12-04)
- The fix sequence: audit first (your page + the #1 and #2 competitors via PageAudit.com), rewrite title / meta / H1 with the keyword "front-loaded where possible" and each tag "better than my two competitors'", then rewrite the body to the competitors' word-count range and density, adding "missing topics my competitors include", without stuffing. (2025-12-08, 2025-12-09)
- Then build 3 incoming internal links into each money page (sitemap + prompt, section 3), and sanity-check every AI suggestion: "make sure the link makes sense in context." (2025-12-10)
- Every blog post must carry "2+ links to service or product pages"; of a post's 3 to 4 internal links "one of them should always link to a service or product page." (2025-07-03)
- Service pages should surface pre-sale answers: Matt is "redesigning some pages on our website to make it easier to find this information", the information being posts that answer "any questions a business owner might have BEFORE we get on a call." (2025-06-30)

### Location / service-area pages
- "service + city combos" are money pages; plan them with the gap analysis; same money-page discipline. (2026-08-04)
- Social profiles can now be GSC properties, which Matt reads as "a great strategy for ranking locally..even without a website! (I do still think websites & website SEO is VERY important)!" (2026-07-09)

### Product and collection pages
- Treated identically to service pages in every prompt ("my product/service page"). Audit, tag rewrite, body rewrite, incoming links all apply. (2025-12-08 to 2025-12-10)

### Pricing pages
- Classed as money pages ("product or pricing pages"). (2026-08-04)
- Transparency rule (from Marcus Sheridan's *Endless Customers*): "be the most transparent business in your industry." Matt published "How Much Does SEO Cost in 2025? (The Brutally Honest Guide)" to "address ALL of your questions about how & why SEO costs what it does" and folds every reader question back into the post. (2025-06-22)

### FAQ pages and FAQ blocks
- FAQs are content pages, not money pages. (2026-08-04)
- Each real customer question is a blog post, not an FAQ line: answer it "Like you'd explain it to a friend over coffee." (2026-05-09)

### Blog posts
- The HeyTony internal Content Checklist (verbatim in section 3): title 60 chars max with keyword near the front; meta 120 to 156 chars with keyword near the front; exactly one H1 with the keyword; several H2s with the keyword in 2 to 3 of them; minimum 1,000 words and at least as long as each of the top 3 results individually; 3 to 4 webp images with keyword-bearing alt text (at least one photo's alt carrying the focus keyword); keyword in the URL; 3 to 4 internal links including one to a service/product page and some to older pages; 1 to 2 external links to non-competitors; 2 to 3 links in from other posts; 2+ links out to service/product pages; a link to the Google Business Profile using the GBP target keyword; cannibalization check; sweep for AI phrases ("delve into", "enter a world of", "Today's digital landscape", "a tapestry", "in conclusion", "in summary", "Skyrocket"). (2025-07-03)
- Formatting for the AI-written draft: "Use H2 and H3 headings only. No horizontal rules, section dividers, tables, callout boxes, or other formatting elements. Just headings, paragraphs, and bullet points where appropriate." Then "Add some internal links, give it a quick edit, add some images, then publish." (2026-07-20)
- Editing: copy into a blank document and "Remove anything that feels like AI fluff or unnecessary numbered lists." (2025-12-07)
- Images: "You can use ChatGPT to generate blog images... or use personal/stock images if you prefer." (2025-12-07)
- Targeting: new posts target "an easy-win keyword you'll actually rank for"; older posts get "Update & Boost" (fresh stats, improved structure, added internal links). (2025-12-04)
- Speed of results claimed: a 30-minute, "99% AI written" post on page 1 in 4 hours (2025-11-24); a voice-mode post at #1 within 24 hours, about 15 minutes of work (2026-05-28); "Multiple people are already ranking #1 on Google for the blog post they wrote on Day 1." (2025-12-11)

### About, contact, portfolio, testimonials, legal, landing, 404, header/footer
- No email covers these. `/privacy-policy/` and author pages are explicitly "junk" in the gap analysis (2026-08-04). For structure and design of these pages use `page-types-master.md` (calls + book).

### Rules that apply to every page
- Accessibility: "can someone who is legally blind use it? Because if they can't, it's likely impacting your Google Rankings!" and "good SEO is build on the exact same foundations as accessibility." (2026-07-16)
- Conversion: "improving your conversion rate by just 1% could double your business." (2025-10-10)
- Keyword before page: pages chasing keywords that "never get searched" or are "so competitive they'll never rank" produce nothing even after years. (2025-09-21)
- 90% of website pages get no Google traffic; the keyword-placement rule is the first fix. (2025-06-19)
- Register the site with Google (Search Console) and submit the sitemap; "Your sitemap is how Google efficiently discovers and indexes your pages." If you cannot find it, "fix that ASAP." (2025-06-26, 2025-12-10)

## 3. The verbatim prompts, checklists, and templates

### 3a. HeyTony internal Content Checklist (2025-07-03, "this is an internal checklist that my team uses!")

```
- Title Tag - 60 characters max (include keyword close to the beginning)
- Meta Description - 156 characters max (120 characters min) include keyword close to the beginning
- 1 Heading 1 Tag (H1) 1 per page, only 1 heading 1 tag per page (make sure it includes the keyword)
- Heading 2 Tags (H2) - several H2 tags per page, include keywords in 2-3 of them.
- Content length is a minimum of 1,000 words - word count should be equal to or more than the top 3 individual search results (not combined)
- Readability should be GREEN & easily readable - Yoast SEO already does this for us. If Yoast is not available then don't worry about this.
- Internal linking - should link to 3-4 other pages on the site (one of them should always link to a service or product page. Try to include older pages and not just newly published ones
- External linking - link to 1-2 other websites that we're not competing with for the keyword
- Include 3-4 images - upload images as webp files (and use unsplash.com if you need to find royalty-free or CANVA PRO) - If website is using Squarespace, then compressed Jpeg should be used.
- Alt text - include keywords on every image, describing what the image is and adding them into the focus keyword if it makes sense. At least 1 of the photos should include the focus keyword.
- Content cannibalization - don't use a focus keyword that another page on the site already ranks for. Check to see if that topic has already been covered. If it has and it's not ranking well, updating the content on that page is best practice.
- URL - include the focus keyword in the URL.
- 2-3 links from other blog pages on your site - do this for every post, and it will take care of your internal linking strategy
- 2+ links to service or product pages
- Link to Google business profile - use gbp client target keyword

Errors to Check For
- Check for any missing punctuation - AI sometimes forgets to add some
- Homophone issues - Read over to check for any misused words that don't make sense
- AI overused words - check for phrases like "delve into" or "enter a world of" and replace them if possible. AI likes to use these, and it can get repetitive. "Today's digital landscape", "a tapestry", "in conclusion", "in summary", "Skyrocket".
```

### 3d. Money page audit, the 5 steps (Rank Week Day 2, 2025-12-08)

```
1. Make a copy of this spreadsheet (The "Money Page Audit" sheet I shared with you.)
2. Choose a money page from your site
   - This is a product or service page you actually want people to buy from or contact you through.
3. Paste that URL into PageAudit.com
   - Run an audit on your page.
   - Copy the important info from the audit and paste it into your spreadsheet.
4. Search your main keyword on Google
   - Type in the keyword you want that page to rank for.
5. Audit your top 2 competitors
   - Open the #1 and #2 ranking pages in Google.
   - Run each one through PageAudit.com.
   - Add their data into the same spreadsheet.
```

### 3f. Internal links into money pages, the 7 steps + prompt (Rank Week Day 4, 2025-12-10)

```
STEP 1: Find 5 "money pages" (your products or services) and add them to Column C of your spreadsheet.
STEP 2: Locate your sitemap. If you can't find it or don't have one, fix that ASAP. Your sitemap is how Google efficiently discovers and indexes your pages.
STEP 3: Use the internal linking prompt to find 3 incoming link opportunities for each money page.
STEP 4: Take the 3 URLs ChatGPT recommends and paste them into Column 1 next to your money page.
STEP 5: Go into your website and edit those pages, adding a link pointing back to your money page. Use your own judgment. Don't blindly copy/paste AI suggestions, make sure the link makes sense in context.
STEP 6: Repeat the entire process for the other 4 money pages.
STEP 7: If you want more rankings (and who doesn't?), repeat this for as many money pages as you want.
```

Prompt:
```

[PASTE SITEMAP]
```

### 3g. Blog post, the 11-step workflow (Rank Week Day 1, 2025-12-07)

```
1. Go to AnswerThePublic.com
2. Type in what you sell
3. Find a question you can answer
4. Go to ChatGPT and paste this prompt:
   "I'm writing a blog post answering this question: [PASTE IN YOUR QUESTION]
   Ask me any questions you'd need to know the answer to in order to create a truly unique, helpful blog post.
   It should include my expertise, experience, case studies, etc. Keep it limited to 10 questions."
5. Answer those 10 questions
6. Then paste this prompt:
   "Write the blog post answering this question using my expertise, experience, case studies, etc.: [PASTE IN YOUR QUESTION]"
7. Copy the blog post into a blank document
8. Remove anything that feels like AI fluff or unnecessary numbered lists
9. Add 3 internal links to other relevant pages on your website
10. Publish it on your site
11. Once published, find 3 other related pages on your site and link to your new post
```
("This helps Google find and rank it faster, internal links are magic.")

### 3i. Topic Gap Analysis (2026-08-04, "Steal your competitors' SEO & Rankings")

Steps: find your sitemap and the competitor's (homepage + `/sitemap.xml`), take both URLs, run Prompt 1, then Prompt 2.

Prompt 1:
```
You are a senior SEO strategist. I'm going to give you two sitemaps: mine and a competitor's. Read both in full.

MY SITE: [your sitemap URL]
COMPETITOR: [their sitemap URL]
MY BUSINESS: [what you do, city/service area]

Do this: 1. Convert every URL in both sitemaps into a plain-English topic. Ignore junk: tag pages, author pages, pagination, /cart/, /privacy-policy/, media attachments. 2. Sort each site's pages into two buckets: - MONEY PAGES: services, service + city combos, industries served, product or pricing pages. Anything someone lands on ready to buy. - CONTENT PAGES: blog posts, guides, FAQs, comparisons. Anything someone lands on while researching. 3. Find the gaps: topics they cover that I don't. Then find the CLUSTERS - places where they have 4+ pages on one theme. A cluster is a bigger signal than a single missing page, because it means they're deliberately going after that topic. 4. Rank the gaps by revenue potential, not search volume. A page that's one step from a phone call beats a page that gets traffic and nothing else. 5. Also tell me my REVERSE gaps: what do I cover that they don't? That's my existing advantage and I want to defend it. Output two tables. MONEY PAGE GAPS: Missing Topic | Suggested URL Slug | Suggested Page Title | Why This Converts | Priority (1-5) CONTENT GAPS: Missing Topic | Cluster It Belongs To | Suggested Title | What Stage Of The Buying Process This Catches | Priority (1-5) Then finish with: the single biggest strategic difference between how these two sites are built, in 3 sentences.
```

Prompt 2:
```
Take the top 3 money page gaps and write me the H1, the meta description, and an outline with H2s for each one. Write for a customer who's comparing two or three local companies right now, not someone doing research.
```

## 4. Other tips and tricks

### Keywords
- "going after the wrong keywords" is "One of the biggest mistakes." (2025-07-28) Matt used to guess: "It's like buying a lottery ticket." Cases: a cruise line with years of no results hit 12,000+ visitors/month 8 months after switching keywords; another client went "$20K/month in revenue to $200K/month" by "targeting keywords that actually gave them a chance to rank." (2025-09-21)
- Start with keywords "they could actually rank for (the ones their competitors were ignoring)." (2025-11-07)
- Niche is an advantage: "That's actually your advantage. Less competition." (2026-03-08)
- "you don't need thousands of visitors per month. Some of our clients only get 500 visitors per month and are booked solid" because "they rank for keywords that actually move the needle." (2025-11-05)
- Tools: HeyKeywords.com ($29/month plan; launch code HTkeywords), Findquestions.com, AnswerThePublic; his $500 side-by-side test of SEranking vs Ubersuggest vs Semrush vs Ahrefs (video youtube.com/watch?v=l0l5noyWbuQ; "it was NOT the most expensive one"). (2026-04-06, 2025-07-15)

### Internal linking and site plumbing
- Google uses internal links to learn "which pages matter most", "how your site is structured", "which pages deserve to rank faster." (2025-12-10)
- Register with Google Search Console (2 minutes), then submit the sitemap. (2025-06-26)
- GSC now accepts Instagram, TikTok, X, YouTube as properties (impressions, clicks, keywords per post): Login > site dropdown > "add property." (2026-07-09)
- GSC now has AI tracking: "they want you to rank in the AI results." (2026-06-04)

### Backlinks
- "Before you can rank, Google needs to trust your site. And the best way to build trust is by getting backlinks." (2025-07-25) But "Backlinks are the worst part of SEO... time consuming and/or expensive", and his test video claims "24,000 visitors per month WITHOUT backlinks." (2025-08-04) Middle position: backlinks are neither dead nor more important than content, and "yes they're still important if you want to rank on AI." (2025-11-19, 2025-11-03)
- A working agency cadence: "8 blog posts per month, 4 backlinks per month" took a client from $20k to $120k+/month. (2025-11-07)

### Google Business Profile and reviews
- Review rules above (3l). Video pointers: "Steal These Google Business Profile SEO Hacks" (2025-09-24), "The Fastest Way To Rank #1 on Google Maps in 2025" (2025-09-19). "Ranking higher on Google Maps can change your business... I've seen it happen... a lot."
- Every page links to the GBP with the target keyword as anchor (Content Checklist).

### AI search / AEO
- "AI search isn't the future... It's the present." (2025-11-28) "AI search is taking over" but "there are still billions of Google searches happening per day (and growing)." (2025-07-01)
- Google's "new intelligent search box": longer, ChatGPT-style searches, "heading towards a 0 click system", "Website traffic is going to PLUMMET." But "If Google is recommending your site in its AI.. that means they trust you. And if they trust you, they'll recommend your products & services." (2026-05-22)
