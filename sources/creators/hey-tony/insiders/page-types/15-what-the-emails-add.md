# What the emails add (the short version)

*Part of the HeyTony page-type research (`page-types/`). The rules every page inherits are in `00-rules-for-every-page.md`; the citation key, sources, and file map are in `README.md`.*

Matt's emails (190 from matt@heytony.ca, Jun 2025 to Aug 2026) are mostly Rank Week, Insiders, and bundle promotions. About 60 carry a tip, and the tips are almost all about process (write the post, audit the money page, link internally) rather than page layout. The full digest, with every prompt and checklist verbatim and a dated index, is `heytony-email-tips.md`. What the emails add to the page chapters above:

- **The page taxonomy, in one line.** Money pages are "services, service + city combos, industries served, product or pricing pages. Anything someone lands on ready to buy." Content pages are "blog posts, guides, FAQs, comparisons. Anything someone lands on while researching." Junk to ignore when mapping a site: "tag pages, author pages, pagination, /cart/, /privacy-policy/, media attachments." [email 2026-08-04]
- **Which money pages to build next.** Run the Topic Gap Analysis against a competitor's sitemap (homepage + `/sitemap.xml`), rank gaps "by revenue potential, not search volume. A page that's one step from a phone call beats a page that gets traffic and nothing else," treat a competitor cluster of "4+ pages on one theme" as the bigger signal, and write each new money page "for a customer who's comparing two or three local companies right now, not someone doing research." [email 2026-08-04]
- **The HeyTony internal Content Checklist** (his team's per-page list): title 60 characters max with the keyword near the front; meta 120 to 156 characters with the keyword near the front; exactly one H1 with the keyword; several H2s with the keyword in 2 to 3 of them; a 1,000-word minimum that is also at least as long as each of the top 3 results individually; readability green in Yoast; 3 to 4 internal links (one always to a service or product page, some to older pages); 1 to 2 external links to non-competitors; 3 to 4 webp images with keyword-bearing alt text, at least one photo's alt carrying the focus keyword; keyword in the URL; no cannibalization (if another page already targets the keyword and is not ranking well, update that page instead); 2 to 3 links in from other posts; 2+ links out to service or product pages; a link to the Google Business Profile with the GBP target keyword as anchor; a sweep for missing punctuation, homophones, and AI phrases ("delve into", "enter a world of", "Today's digital landscape", "a tapestry", "in conclusion", "in summary", "Skyrocket"). [email 2025-07-03]
- **The keyword-placement floor for any page.** Title tag, meta description, H1, at least one H2, the content at least twice, the URL if you can. "If you do that, you'll be better off than 90% of websites out there!" because "The most common reason a page doesn't rank is because it doesn't include the keyword they want to rank for.. and even if it does, it's usually mentioned once!" [email 2025-06-19]
- **The money-page checklist in five words.** Title tag, meta description, headings, content improvements, internal links; audit first (your page plus the #1 and #2 results through PageAudit.com on 12 fields), then rewrite the tags so the keyword is "front-loaded where possible" and each tag is "better than my two competitors'", then rewrite the body to the competitors' word-count range and density without stuffing, then add 3 incoming internal links per money page from the sitemap. [emails 2025-12-04, 2025-12-08, 2025-12-09, 2025-12-10]
- **Blog post formatting for AI drafts.** "Use H2 and H3 headings only. No horizontal rules, section dividers, tables, callout boxes, or other formatting elements. Just headings, paragraphs, and bullet points where appropriate." Then strip "AI fluff or unnecessary numbered lists", add internal links and images, publish, and link in from 3 older posts. [emails 2026-07-20, 2025-12-07]
- **Update & Boost for old pages.** Fresh stats, improved structure, added internal links; 5 to 10 strategic internal links per sweep. [email 2025-12-04]
- **Pricing transparency.** "be the most transparent business in your industry" (from *Endless Customers*): publish the cost guide ("How Much Does SEO Cost in 2025? (The Brutally Honest Guide)"), fold reader questions back into it, and write the posts that answer "any questions a business owner might have BEFORE we get on a call", then redesign pages so that information is easy to find. [emails 2025-06-22, 2025-06-30]
- **Accessibility is an SEO foundation.** "can someone who is legally blind use it? Because if they can't, it's likely impacting your Google Rankings!" [email 2026-07-16]
- **Conversion.** "improving your conversion rate by just 1% could double your business." [email 2025-10-10]
- **Review solicitation rules (GBP).** No incentives, no staff names, ask everyone not just happy customers, do not ask on premises, do not ask for specific content. [email 2026-04-16]
- **The counter-example to over-building.** A one-page, 35-word, vibe-coded site with no meta description and no H2s ranks #1 "for a ton of keywords" (the why was behind a link). Read it as "intent and niche beat polish", not as a page spec. [email 2026-08-11]
- **Legal pages.** The only mention: `/privacy-policy/` is junk to ignore when mapping topics, meaning it is not an SEO page. [email 2026-08-04]
- **What the emails never cover:** homepage structure, about, contact, portfolio, testimonials page layout, landing pages, 404/thank-you pages, header/footer. Use the chapters above for those.
