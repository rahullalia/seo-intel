### 2026-08-04, Steal your competitors' SEO & Rankings (prompt) (19fce54c83d320f8)

- Strategy name: "Topic Gap Analysis", described as "a great way to find out what your competitors are doing that you're not."
- How to find a sitemap: "Go to your home page and add /sitemap.xml - that will show you a page with a bunch of links on it." Do this for your site and the competitor's site, and take both sitemap URLs.
- Page taxonomy the prompt enforces (useful as a page-type definition):
  - MONEY PAGES: "services, service + city combos, industries served, product or pricing pages. Anything someone lands on ready to buy."
  - CONTENT PAGES: "blog posts, guides, FAQs, comparisons. Anything someone lands on while researching."
  - Junk to ignore: "tag pages, author pages, pagination, /cart/, /privacy-policy/, media attachments."
- Cluster rule: "find the CLUSTERS - places where they have 4+ pages on one theme. A cluster is a bigger signal than a single missing page, because it means they're deliberately going after that topic."
- Prioritization rule: "Rank the gaps by revenue potential, not search volume. A page that's one step from a phone call beats a page that gets traffic and nothing else."
- Reverse gaps: "what do I cover that they don't? That's my existing advantage and I want to defend it."
- Money page gap output columns: Missing Topic | Suggested URL Slug | Suggested Page Title | Why This Converts | Priority (1-5).
- Content gap output columns: Missing Topic | Cluster It Belongs To | Suggested Title | What Stage Of The Buying Process This Catches | Priority (1-5).
- Follow-up prompt builds the top 3 money pages: H1, meta description, and an outline with H2s for each, written "for a customer who's comparing two or three local companies right now, not someone doing research."
- Matt's framing of the payoff: "Congratulations, you just saved yourself thousands of dollars, and have a killer SEO strategy."
- Step list, verbatim (numbering as in the email):

```
1. Find your sitemap & your competitors site map. Go to your home page and add /sitemap.xml - that will show you a page with a bunch of links on it.

2. Take the url to your sitemap & your competitors sitemap.

3. Use this prompt:
[prompt 1 below]

4. When it's done, use this prompt:
[prompt 2 below]

5. Congratulations, you just saved yourself thousands of dollars, and have a killer SEO strategy.
```
