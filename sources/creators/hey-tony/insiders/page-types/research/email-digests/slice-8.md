### 2025-07-03, , your content checklist! (197d20673505ed74)

- "Here's a quick checklist you can use to make sure your pages are optimized and primed to rank on Google!" PS: "this is an internal checklist that my team uses! Enjoy!"
- The link resolves to a public Google Doc titled "Content Checklist": https://docs.google.com/document/d/1F_WTyyTufGdFZy3-ZOoOg0E6-ygyY21LGhKoSqRH6pM/edit
- Full checklist, verbatim (doc dashes normalized to hyphens):
  - Title Tag - 60 characters max (include keyword close to the beginning)
  - Meta Description - 156 characters max (120 characters min) include keyword close to the beginning
  - 1 Heading 1 Tag (H1) 1 per page, only 1 heading 1 tag per page (make sure it includes the keyword)
  - Heading 2 Tags (H2) - several H2 tags per page, include keywords in 2-3 of them.
  - Content length is a minimum of 1,000 words - word count should be equal to or more than the top 3 individual search results (not combined)
  - Readability should be GREEN & easily readable - Yoast SEO already does this for us. If Yoast is not available then don't worry about this.
  - Internal linking - should link to 3-4 other pages on the site (one of them should always link to a service or product page. Try to include older pages and not just newly published ones
  - External linking - link to 1-2 other websites that we're not competing with for the keyword
  - Include 3-4 images - upload images as webp files (and use unsplash.com if you need to find royalty-free or CANVA PRO) - If website is using Squarespace, then compressed Jpeg should be used.
  - Alt text - include keywords on every image, describing what the image is and adding them into the focus keyword if it makes sense. At least 1 of the photos should include the focus keyword.
  - Content cannibalization - don't use a focus keyword that another page on the site already ranks for. Check to see if that topic has already been covered. If it has and it's not ranking well, updating the content on that page is best practice.
  - URL - include the focus keyword in the URL.
  - 2-3 links from other blog pages on your site - do this for every post, and it will take care of your internal linking strategy
  - 2+ links to service or product pages
  - Link to Google business profile - use gbp client target keyword
- "Errors to Check For" section, verbatim:
  - Check for any missing punctuation - AI sometimes forgets to add some
  - Homophone issues - Read over to check for any misused words that don't make sense
  - AI overused words - check for phrases like "delve into" or "enter a world of" and replace them if possible. AI likes to use these, and it can get repetitive. "Today's digital landscape", "a tapestry", "in conclusion", "in summary", "Skyrocket".
