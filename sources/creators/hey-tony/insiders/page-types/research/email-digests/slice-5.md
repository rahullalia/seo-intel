### 2025-12-07, Rank Week Day 1: Recap & Replay (19afa6857bcafc6d)

Theme: writing and publishing a question-answering blog post in one sitting, with AI doing the drafting and the business owner supplying the expertise.

- Offer: join HeyTony Insiders with code RANKWEEK10 for $10 off the first month; "This code is only available TODAY" (Dec 7). Insiders also get all the bonuses given away during the week.
- Outcome of the live session: "seeing so many of you publish a blog post during the session is wild." So many attendees hit AnswerThePublic.com at once that "We even managed to crash AnswerThePublic.com... yes, the entire site." Matt messaged Neil Patel to tell him.
- The exact 11-step blog workflow, copied in full:
  1. Go to AnswerThePublic.com
  2. Type in what you sell
  3. Find a question you can answer
  4. Go to ChatGPT and paste this prompt:
     > "I'm writing a blog post answering this question: [PASTE IN YOUR QUESTION]
     > Ask me any questions you'd need to know the answer to in order to create a truly unique, helpful blog post.
     > It should include my expertise, experience, case studies, etc. Keep it limited to 10 questions."
  5. Answer those 10 questions
  6. Then paste this prompt:
     > "Write the blog post answering this question using my expertise, experience, case studies, etc.: [PASTE IN YOUR QUESTION]"
  7. Copy the blog post into a blank document
  8. Remove anything that feels like AI fluff or unnecessary numbered lists
  9. Add 3 internal links to other relevant pages on your website
  10. Publish it on your site
  11. Once published, find 3 other related pages on your site and link to your new post
  (His note on step 11: "This helps Google find and rank it faster, internal links are magic.")
- Blog topic source: a real question people ask (AnswerThePublic), not an invented topic. You pick "a question you can answer."
- Blog content source: the AI interviews YOU first (10 questions max) so the post contains "my expertise, experience, case studies, etc." and is "truly unique, helpful."
- Editing rule for AI drafts: strip "AI fluff" and "unnecessary numbered lists" before publishing.
- Internal links on a blog post: 3 outbound links from the new post to relevant pages on your site, plus 3 inbound links from related existing pages to the new post, added after publishing.
- Images: "You can use ChatGPT to generate blog images… or use personal/stock images if you prefer."
- Mindset: "SEO is just consistent tiny wins stacked over time." "A ton of you clicked 'publish' for the first time, and that momentum is everything."

- Google relies on internal links to understand: "which pages matter most", "how your site is structured", "which pages deserve to rank faster."
- The exact 7-step process, copied in full:
  - STEP 1: Find 5 "money pages" (your products or services) and add them to Column C of your spreadsheet.
  - STEP 2: Locate your sitemap. It's usually at: [tracked link in the email; destination not visible in the plain-text body]. If you can't find it or don't have one, fix that ASAP. Your sitemap is how Google efficiently discovers and indexes your pages.
  - STEP 3: Use the internal linking prompt (copied below) to find 3 incoming link opportunities for each money page:
    > I'm looking for 3 incoming internal linking opportunities to this service/product page: [LINK TO PAGE IN COLUMN C]
    >
    > Find me 3 relevant opportunities from my sitemap pasted below.
    > [PASTE SITEMAP]
  - STEP 4: Take the 3 URLs ChatGPT recommends and paste them into Column 1 next to your money page.
  - STEP 5: Go into your website and edit those pages, adding a link pointing back to your money page. "And this is important: Use your own judgment. Don't blindly copy/paste AI suggestions, make sure the link makes sense in context."
  - STEP 6: Repeat the entire process for the other 4 money pages.
  - STEP 7: If you want more rankings (and who doesn't?), repeat this for as many money pages as you want.
- Numbers: 5 money pages per pass, 3 incoming internal links per money page (15 new links minimum), then keep going for as many money pages as you want.
- Direction of links: existing pages (found via the sitemap) link TO the money page; the money page is the target, not the source.
- "Internal linking = SEO momentum." "Internal linking is one of those strategies that separates websites that rank consistently from websites that sit on page 5 forever."
