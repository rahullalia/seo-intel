## 3. Location / city / service-area pages

- When expanding to new states or areas, "right in your navigation, you would say locations or service areas and just start listing each of those different areas" as location pages. [097_technical-seo-with-denroi-15-01-2026.txt]
- Florida dumpster company: build "Port St. Lucie dumpster rental" as its own page; spell it the way people search ("St." not "Saint"; all the volume sits on "St.") and use that spelling on the page several times. Service area menu had cities, counties and dumpster sizes nested under each: "There's way too much going on... how do you even do this on mobile?... I can't even click those. So why are they there?" Rule: "service area. I want to see one drop down. You could have 10 on here if you want to"; no county pages unless the keyword tool shows people search the county ("Indian River" had almost nothing, so no page). [103_wdir-02-01-2026-replay.txt]
- Kira, junk car buyer with 7 service areas and 3 neighborhoods each (no physical location): homepage keyword attaches to the state, city pages use city plus state or city only depending on volume ("we buy junk cars Dallas" 210/mo, KD 9 versus 20/mo with "Texas"), neighborhood pages use keyword plus neighborhood plus city, not the state. Same root phrase across cities is not cannibalization because each city is a different keyword, "as long as we're not shooting for the same keyword and that the content doesn't feel the same." [107_content-writing-training-with-kira-12-23-2025.txt]
- How location pages are written (Kira, the method Matt has the team use): "we're talking more about the location... Oh, you're in Dallas. Like, let's talk a little bit about Dallas... Buying a junk car in Houston is helpful because Houston has this and that and Dallas has that. Now we have differentiated pages." [107_content-writing-training-with-kira-12-23-2025.txt]

Even when the service is identical except a fee, "just change up the wording... it's not like Calgary web design would be very much different than designing a website for Regina or Saskatoon. It's all the same product. They just changed up the lingo for each of these service pages to target a different location." Example site: a web design agency with a "serving clients in" list of cities, each its own page, plus industry pages: "take one topic and create a page about it." [106_web-design-for-seo-with-leanne-jones-12-24-2025-replay.txt]

- "Northwest Lien" going national: keep the domain, "create a location page and see if that drives any traffic"; study how a bigger multi-region company structures its site and copy or improve. [105_wdir-26-12-2025-replay.txt]
- Toronto movers: "movers Toronto" is 62% difficulty; neighborhood pages (North York, Leslieville, Queen West, Yorkville) are "a really great keyword" but need authority, blog content and topical authority first; the site already had location pages in the footer. [101_wdir-09-01-2026-replay.txt]

## 15. General page rules that apply to every page

Keywords, titles, headings
- "You want to rank for one keyword per page. You want to focus on that singular keyword per page." Secondary keywords are variations to prove you cover the topic; ask AI for 10; do not research their volume. [097_technical-seo-with-denroi-15-01-2026.txt] [107_content-writing-training-with-kira-12-23-2025.txt]
- Title tag: main keyword at the beginning, brand at the end; meta description 140 to 145 characters with the keyword toward the front; Yoast SEO title field in WordPress, "search engine listing" in Shopify. [097_technical-seo-with-denroi-15-01-2026.txt] [105_wdir-26-12-2025-replay.txt] [106_web-design-for-seo-with-leanne-jones-12-24-2025-replay.txt]
- One H1, first on the page, containing the keyword; H2s reinforce the H1; H3s support H2s; do not jump H1 to H5 or H2 to H6 ("It should go to H4"); going backwards (H5 to H2) is fine; heading tags used for styling is wrong outside ad landing pages; "four H1 tags and an H2 at the start, that's kind of when it gets a little bit confusing for Google." [098_choosing-strategic-colors-for-your-website-replay-14-01-2026.txt] [103_wdir-02-01-2026-replay.txt] [105_wdir-26-12-2025-replay.txt] [097_technical-seo-with-denroi-15-01-2026.txt]
- Duplicate hidden sections for desktop and mobile (Wix, Squarespace, GoHighLevel builders) produce two H1s and repeated H2s; use a truly responsive theme (Avada, Astra with Elementor/Divi) that reflows instead of duplicating; if you must duplicate, keep the tags identical. The Page Audit extension flags multiple H1s but not repeated H2s. [105_wdir-26-12-2025-replay.txt]
- Keyword usage: more than once per page; match the top result's count plus one; Page Audit tool shows occurrences and density; no stuffing ("Why Rainbow City colorful cute combat boots"), no hidden same-color text, no keyword in hover text. [096_wdir-replay-16-01-2026.txt] [102_work-session-writing-a-blog-post-05-01-2026-replay.txt] [097_technical-seo-with-denroi-15-01-2026.txt] [103_wdir-02-01-2026-replay.txt] [105_wdir-26-12-2025-replay.txt]
- URL: contains keyword and location (compactor-rentals-new-jersey); "M+M" changed to "M and M" because "Google doesn't like characters" ("That's fine"). [105_wdir-26-12-2025-replay.txt] [096_wdir-replay-16-01-2026.txt]
- "Key takeaways" on a service page "tells me that this was written by ChatGPT"; on blog posts it is encouraged. [105_wdir-26-12-2025-replay.txt] [103_wdir-02-01-2026-replay.txt]

Length and density
- Match the top three and add a little (800 to 805, 1,500 to 1,550); 2,800 words against 823 is too much; Leanne's default is 1,000 to 1,500 words per page; a 1,270-word service page is "a good word count"; a 2,600-word post is "a bit long, but it's okay." [105_wdir-26-12-2025-replay.txt] [102_work-session-writing-a-blog-post-05-01-2026-replay.txt] [106_web-design-for-seo-with-leanne-jones-12-24-2025-replay.txt] [107_content-writing-training-with-kira-12-23-2025.txt] [104_work-session-writing-a-blog-post-29-12-2025-replay.txt]
- "Sometimes less is more... think of the user... Do they need all this information?" Text is important for SEO but nobody reads walls; "people will read the headlines before they start reading big walls of text... break it up into digestible sections." [105_wdir-26-12-2025-replay.txt] [100_web-design-for-seo-07-01-2026-replay.txt]

Images and video
- WebP everywhere (PNG and JPEG to WebP; free converter sites cap around 10 a day; Imagify/WP Rocket plugins on WordPress; Photoshop export at about 70%); "webp will give you a high quality photo and just have a smaller file size." [096_wdir-replay-16-01-2026.txt] [100_web-design-for-seo-07-01-2026-replay.txt] [106_web-design-for-seo-with-leanne-jones-12-24-2025-replay.txt] [104_work-session-writing-a-blog-post-29-12-2025-replay.txt]
- Pixel size drives file size: design desktop at about 1,400 px wide and never save a full-width image wider than that; a half-width image is at most about 600 px; measure the rendered width (about 540 px) and save at that size; a 1,200 px image in a 650 px slot is too big because "your server will still have to render this huge picture and then just squish it down." [100_web-design-for-seo-07-01-2026-replay.txt]
- Add missing image dimensions (WP Rocket option) to clear the PageSpeed flag. [096_wdir-replay-16-01-2026.txt]
- Lazy loading images is good practice. [100_web-design-for-seo-07-01-2026-replay.txt]
- Images should be crisp, not blurry or cut off, cohesive in style; real photos over stock; AI-generated images are acceptable (labelled dumpster, featured images). [105_wdir-26-12-2025-replay.txt] [100_web-design-for-seo-07-01-2026-replay.txt]
- A picture in every section helps people "quickly understand the message"; icons for 1-2-3 steps; choose images that match the headline (house icon for residential moves); "kids prefer to read a picture book because it makes it easier to read." [100_web-design-for-seo-07-01-2026-replay.txt]
- Video backgrounds: avoid unless branding demands it ("if it helps with conversions, it might be worth it"); turn off on mobile; add a colored overlay or black-to-transparent gradient (multiply blend) so white text is readable; Vimeo gives a clean embedded player; a 2 MB over-compressed hero video looks pixelated. [098_choosing-strategic-colors-for-your-website-replay-14-01-2026.txt] [100_web-design-for-seo-07-01-2026-replay.txt] [103_wdir-02-01-2026-replay.txt]
- Remove image backgrounds (Photoshop remove background, crop, export PNG with transparency; Canva Pro background remover) when layering a cut-out over a hero. [106_web-design-for-seo-with-leanne-jones-12-24-2025-replay.txt]

Speed and mobile
- Mobile comes first: "What matters most to user experience is how it loads on mobile because people are less patient on their phones"; Google has done mobile-first indexing since about 2016; a site that is not responsive when the window shrinks "isn't great for the user experience." Target at least 80 on mobile PageSpeed. [100_web-design-for-seo-07-01-2026-replay.txt] [105_wdir-26-12-2025-replay.txt] [096_wdir-replay-16-01-2026.txt]

Readability, color, fonts, layout
- Contrast: baby pink and cream backgrounds, light gray thin text, same-color nav links and barely visible blue links all failed; "go white or go fully black"; "when in doubt, black, just go black with everything." [096_wdir-replay-16-01-2026.txt] [103_wdir-02-01-2026-replay.txt] [097_technical-seo-with-denroi-15-01-2026.txt]
