## 2. Service pages (parent/overview and individual child service pages) and money pages in general

- A long tail keyword like "Catholic natural family planning online classes" "needs a dedicated page for this keyword". [119_08-12-2025-replay.txt]
- Every page on the site should target one keyword or one small group of variations; do not optimize more than one page for the same topic: "these guys are trying to double dip. We're only going to show the one page... None of them are gonna rank." [125_seo-live-training.txt] Same rule stated to a new cleaning business: "every single page on your website should be focused on one keyword or one small group of keywords." [118_rank-week-day-2-replay.txt]

## 4. Product, collection, category pages (ecommerce)

So if you can make sure it's not in a dropdown menu, that's fine"; text below "all of the important information that people need to buy, check out, look at the product" is fine.
