## 4. Product, collection, category pages (ecommerce)

- Matt Demers' ecommerce on-page checklist: keyword in the product name (usually the H1), SEO/meta title, meta description, URL slug, the first 100 words of the product description, naturally throughout, and in at least one subheading; plus image alt text and image file names. [041_ecommerce-w-matt-demers-19-05-2026.txt]
- Collection vs product keywords: "you're usually going to go broad on your collection page and go more specific on your product pages"; collection = "paddle T-shirts"; products carry attributes (polyester paddle shirts, silk paddle shirts, lightweight, moisture wicking); "your collection pages hold the most weight too typically." [041_ecommerce-w-matt-demers-19-05-2026.txt]
- Shopify product pages usually have no place for extra headings, so add an FAQ section (theme block usually called "collapsible row" / "collapsible content"), set the rows to specific heading types, use keywords in them; FAQs give context for long-tail AI searches ("everyone's not typing in just four words anymore... what temperature they play"); start with collection pages because doing every product is a lot of work. [041_ecommerce-w-matt-demers-19-05-2026.txt]
- FAQs also help conversion: "people often have questions that stop them from purchasing... addressing them can help with conversion," plus keyword opportunity and "good for the bots to know the differences between your product and others." [041_ecommerce-w-matt-demers-19-05-2026.txt]
- Image file names: Google reads the file name; upload "black-flame-paddle-shirt" not "1234.jpg"; lower priority but "we only have so many places to optimize"; people "shop with their eyes" and may be on the images tab; keyword in file name + alt text + on page = controlling every variable. Tools: Image Alt Text Viewer; On Page SEO AI (shows all alt text and file names in one place, generates titles/meta). [041_ecommerce-w-matt-demers-19-05-2026.txt]

## 15. General page rules that apply to every page (length, density, images, headings, speed, mobile, readability, AEO/answer-first) when framed as page-building guidance

- Length benchmark: 10% more than the longest top-3 competitor; never 2-3x (over-optimization). If competitors have 500 words and you have 3,000, "maybe you need to be closer to 500." [030_techinical-seo-with-denroi-11-06-2026.txt] [032_wdir-05-06-2026.txt]
- Keyword count: beat each competitor variation by one or two; local keywords often need only two or three insertions; also benchmark heading count, internal links, backlinks and descriptive anchor text. [030_techinical-seo-with-denroi-11-06-2026.txt]
- One keyword per page; one root keyword with natural variations in the body; do not target the same keyword on two pages (cannibalization: Google picks one page per site per keyword). [035_technical-seo-with-elliot-27-05-2026.txt] [037_work-session-25-05-2026.txt]
- Long-tail is the direction: David went from 709 to 3,600 organic keywords by answering longer questions, many with 50-100% CTR at low volume; people "are doing crazier searches, longer searches, more specific searches." [038_wdir-05-22-2026.txt]
- Answer-first and human-written: "directly answer the question at the top"; "it needs to be written for humans because AI is looking at how valuable that content is to humans"; "do more of what works, not more of what people say works." [038_wdir-05-22-2026.txt]
- Support the AI's claim first, then differentiate (section 9). [031_work-session-press-releases-08-06-2026.txt]
- Do not block AI crawlers: "if you're getting cited by AI, that means Google trusts your website"; blocking "is the stupidest thing I've ever heard"; if bots crash the site, upgrade hosting (Matt pays about $30/month at Crocweb) and add a WAF. [038_wdir-05-22-2026.txt] [034_wdir-29-05-2026.txt]
- Headings: exactly one H1; a big font does not make an H1, check the semantic tag (Wix: text > SEO and accessibility > HTML tag; Squarespace forces H1 to the global H1 style, a known con); buttons, stat blocks and descriptive text should be paragraph text not headings; "items added to your cart"/related products should not be H2; empty H1/H2 tags (common Shopify Dawn injection) should be fixed in theme code. [036_web-design-for-seo-27-05-2026.txt] [035_technical-seo-with-elliot-27-05-2026.txt] [041_ecommerce-w-matt-demers-19-05-2026.txt]
- Title and meta: SEO title under 60 characters; meta description roughly 140-155, under 160; exact keyword in both (it is fine to push naturalness there); titles/metas are edited in Rank Math / Yoast in the WordPress back end (also available in Elementor page settings). [041_ecommerce-w-matt-demers-19-05-2026.txt] [035_technical-seo-with-elliot-27-05-2026.txt] [036_web-design-for-seo-27-05-2026.txt]
- URL/slug: keyword in the slug; keep slugs when rebuilding; changing a slug needs a 301 and even then traffic sometimes does not carry over; clone pages in WordPress then update the title and slug so you don't get "about-2". [041_ecommerce-w-matt-demers-19-05-2026.txt] [034_wdir-29-05-2026.txt] [036_web-design-for-seo-27-05-2026.txt]
