## 15. General page rules that apply to every page (length, density, images, headings, speed, mobile, readability, AEO/answer-first) when framed as page-building guidance

- Heading hierarchy: "We should only have one H1. This is our title of our page. And then we can have as many H2s and H3s as we want. They just should be in this order": H1, then H2 per section, H3s as subtopics inside; "I wouldn't go any deeper than H3"; H4 only for sub-sub detail. [067_web-design-for-seo-18-03-2026.txt]
- H1 placement: "we want the H1 tag to be first... It's like the title of the book. You don't have a secondary title before the book starts." The H1 is typically the biggest text, but in Elementor/WordPress you can style it smaller. [067_web-design-for-seo-18-03-2026.txt] Tony: "H1 tag always the very top." [066_why-doesn-t-it-rank-20-03-2026.txt]
- "Your H1 is the title of the book... the H2 is the chapter title... H2s are the chapter titles. And that supports what the book is about." Six H1s and 30 H2s: "Is that terrible? Yes." How many H2s: "Look at what your competitors are doing and do one more than them." H3s: "there's no rule that says you need to have heading three tags." [071_rank-week-2026-day5.txt] [069_work-session-16-03-2026.txt]
- H2s should be short chapter titles that support the H1: "Human Design Business readings and group programs for heart led entrepreneurs who want to create an epic offer" is "a very long H2 tag and that doesn't support the H1"; "to skyrocket your big vision" as an H3 "was written by AI 100%"; keep that text as a paragraph, not a heading: "I thought I need to put H2 immediately... that's not the truth." [070_wdir-13-03-2026.txt]
- Don't skip levels: an H2 jumping to H4 should be H2 then H3s (nannies, family assistants, newborn care specialists, babysitting). [068_technical-seo-with-denroi-19-03-2026.txt]
- Why headings matter: "it helps explain to Google what your page is about in the shortest time possible. If Google's quickly looking over at your page, it will look at just your heading tags first." Section-based model: every section = one topic = one H2; sub-points = H3. [067_web-design-for-seo-18-03-2026.txt]
- Designers often get tags wrong: "Sometimes developers and designers aren't paying attention to the H tags. They just design it visually correct, but it's not technically correct", e.g. a paragraph tag styled like a heading; in Elementor use the Heading element and set the HTML tag; turn off the automatic page title in page settings to avoid a second H1. [067_web-design-for-seo-18-03-2026.txt]
- Keyword placement: title tag, meta description, H1, maybe one H2, and a couple of times in the content. [066_why-doesn-t-it-rank-20-03-2026.txt] Tools only count the exact phrase; variations read naturally. [066_why-doesn-t-it-rank-20-03-2026.txt]
- Keyword frequency: copy what the ranking competitor does; a niche keyword may need only two or three mentions; 10 is likely too many; Yoast's five-times rule can over-optimize. [071_rank-week-2026-day5.txt] [070_wdir-13-03-2026.txt]
- "Every page should be about one individual topic"; "One keyword is for one page"; photography/photographer/photos are the same keyword; three pages on one topic cannibalize and "Google will be like, hey, you already have a page about this topic... so they don't index the other one". [065_work-session-23-03-2026.txt] [070_wdir-13-03-2026.txt]
- Keyword variations can share a page; adding a location makes a new keyword and a new page. [069_work-session-16-03-2026.txt]
- Word count: match the top three competitors, not a fixed number; long-tail pages may need 400 words; a 9,000-word service page is far too long; check where competitors' hidden words come from. [066_why-doesn-t-it-rank-20-03-2026.txt] [070_wdir-13-03-2026.txt]
- Readability (Flesch): "try to keep it like two to three sentences per paragraph. And if you can, don't only use long sentences." [071_rank-week-2026-day5.txt]
- Answer-first for AI: first sentence answers the question directly; key takeaways bullets at the end; AI tools scan whole pages and write their own summary which you cannot change (it is not your meta description). [065_work-session-23-03-2026.txt] [072_technical-seo-with-denroi-13-03-2026.txt]
- Title tags and meta descriptions: missing or unoptimized ones are the first fix on nearly every audited site; the Money Page Doctor / page audit flags "add [keyword] to your page title" and "add a clear call to action button". [068_technical-seo-with-denroi-19-03-2026.txt] [066_why-doesn-t-it-rank-20-03-2026.txt]
- Every section gets a CTA and every page ends with one; CTA text should be specific and carry the keyword ("Shop Organic Dog Treats", "Learn more about Design Art Camp", "Book an appointment"), never generic "learn more" / "shop now". [067_web-design-for-seo-18-03-2026.txt] [068_technical-seo-with-denroi-19-03-2026.txt] [072_technical-seo-with-denroi-13-03-2026.txt]
- Design process: wireframe/design first (Figma is industry standard, Adobe XD, or Canva), then build; "it's not a necessary step but I find it helps". Design desktop, then mobile. [067_web-design-for-seo-18-03-2026.txt]
- Whitespace and spacing: "for design purposes, we'd like to have some white space at the top and at the bottom. This makes it look more digestible... it also improves readability"; section padding 2em top and bottom (about 50px), ~50px between elements, ~25px margin/gap between grid items; padding consistent top and bottom "adds consistency to your design". [067_web-design-for-seo-18-03-2026.txt]
- Section backgrounds: alternate a light brand color and white "to really help visually divide each section". [067_web-design-for-seo-18-03-2026.txt]
- Colors: choose from the brand guideline, or pull from the logo (teal, gold, green example); use one accent color (gold) for CTA buttons; make sure text stays readable (white text on dark imagery). [067_web-design-for-seo-18-03-2026.txt]
- Fonts in the demo: Noto Serif 64px / line-height 64 for the H1, Open Sans 20px for H3 card titles (Google fonts). [067_web-design-for-seo-18-03-2026.txt]
- Hero build: background image container, min-height about 80vh (100vh for full screen), no repeat, size cover; keep text live on top of the image, never baked into the image ("we want to make sure that our text is live"). [067_web-design-for-seo-18-03-2026.txt]
- Hover/alt text is not a substitute for live text: "It won't read that as your H1 tag. It'll read that as alt text, which isn't as powerful as having live text." [067_web-design-for-seo-18-03-2026.txt]
- Icons: buy SVG icons (Creative Market) so you can recolor them to the brand. [067_web-design-for-seo-18-03-2026.txt]
- Image file names: descriptive, four to five words, e.g. "woman-receiving-relaxing-massage.jpeg"; the focus keyword does not need to be in every file name; alt text includes the focus keyword only where it genuinely fits (a massage photo yes, a heart icon "heart icon in a teal color" no); don't bother much with the image title field. [067_web-design-for-seo-18-03-2026.txt]

- Converting to webp: WordPress plugin does it automatically; Squarespace/Wix: download, batch with Photoshop actions or Squoosh, re-upload. [066_why-doesn-t-it-rank-20-03-2026.txt]
- Videos: never host on your own site; upload to YouTube/Vimeo/Dailymotion and embed. [068_technical-seo-with-denroi-19-03-2026.txt] Hero videos slow the page and can look broken; prefer an image. [066_why-doesn-t-it-rank-20-03-2026.txt]
- Speed: a WordPress site can be fast (WP Rocket or LiteSpeed cache, proper image sizes, "not using too many animations and videos and too many embedded elements such as your Instagram feed"); custom-coded is fastest but must stay editable by the client. [067_web-design-for-seo-18-03-2026.txt]
- Lazy/animated loading hurts: "I hate that your site loads like this... nothing is loading right away"; Squarespace animation timings should be cut in half because Google's crawler scrolls and "if it just sees blank screen before anything's loaded, you can have some issues". - URLs: don't change URLs after they rank; if you must, set up 301 redirects (redirect plugin on WordPress). [067_web-design-for-seo-18-03-2026.txt]
- Redesign/domain change: keep page content and URLs as similar as possible for pages with traffic; a domain change means "you're starting from scratch" (losing backlinks and rankings). [065_work-session-23-03-2026.txt]

- Schema: Yoast's toggle covers standard schema on all page types; specialized schema (reviews, prices, FAQ) needs third-party tools; lower priority than content; visible broken schema text at the page bottom must be fixed or removed. [072_technical-seo-with-denroi-13-03-2026.txt] [070_wdir-13-03-2026.txt]
- Noindex: avoid on pages you may want to rank later (backdating). [068_technical-seo-with-denroi-19-03-2026.txt]
- Internal linking: anchor text should describe the destination; first links highest on the page weigh most; every post links to a money page; the hub/services page links down to every child. [065_work-session-23-03-2026.txt] [072_technical-seo-with-denroi-13-03-2026.txt] [068_technical-seo-with-denroi-19-03-2026.txt]
