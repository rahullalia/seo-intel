## 2. Service pages (parent/overview and individual child service pages) and money pages in general

## 9. Blog index and blog post pages (page structure, length, images, headings, CTAs; not topic selection)

- Blog index page must not show the entire post: Linda's blog page included full articles inline; "Don't do that. Just include like, the picture and the thumbnail and then they can click through to read the next thing." Otherwise Google "looks at this page and then they'll click on the blog post. And they'll say, well, all this information is already on another page, so why am I going to index that?" Use the builder's excerpt/listing blocks. [043_wdir-15-05-2026.txt]
- Blog posts on the homepage: show three, no heavy background photo. [049_wdir-01-05-2026.txt]
- TL;DR / key takeaways at the very top of long posts (aiplusinfo.com, 1,730 articles, Deep Brew post used the keyword 91 times): "Google will crawl maybe the first 500 words of your blog before it deems it, if it's necessary to index or if it's not"; if the opening is too heavy Google "won't go through the rest of your blog unfortunately, no matter like how great that is written." Move key takeaways and the table of contents above the deep content; be concise at the start. [044_technical-seo-with-elliot-14-05-2026.txt]
- Long-tail question keyword on a blog post: repeat the question "like three or four times throughout the content, including like your title tag, your H1 tag, your meta description, and probably once in your content." Yoast/Rank Math warnings are built for three or four word short-tail keywords; "I don't care about that." [051_work-session-27-04-2026.txt]
- Title tag and meta description for posts: just ask Claude or ChatGPT "write me a title tag and a meta description"; "make sure the keyword's in both or the question's in both." [046_work-session-11-05-2026.txt]
- Title length: "aim for, like, 60 characters or less." Carol's 81-character titles: leave them and revisit in three or four months if those posts do nothing. [048_work-session-04-05-2026.txt]
- Headings in a post: the H1 is the post title/question; body headings should be H2s; "go in sequential order. So H1 will go into an H2 and an H2 can go into an H3, but you can't go from an H1 to an H3. Or you can, but you shouldn't." If you prefer the look of H3s, restyle the H2s to look like them and make H3s slightly smaller. [048_work-session-04-05-2026.txt]
- Images: "I try to do it every two to 300 words"; size about 750 wide by 400-500 tall; real photos beat everything ("nobody else can make those real pictures"), screenshots of what you are talking about are 100% relevant, AI-generated images are OK "as long as it adds to the content and it's not just a random picture"; brand colors yes. Image filenames: "Just be descriptive of what's actually in the picture. Don't worry about adding the keyword a bunch of times." [048_work-session-04-05-2026.txt]
- Internal links in every new post: "find three other blog posts on your site... that are relevant to that post and find words that you can link to those other pages for... then you're going to grab a link to one of your money pages, so product or service." After publishing, go into three older posts or pages that mention the topic and link to the new post (add a paragraph if needed); that helps Google find it, sometimes "within like an hour or two." "The more times you're linking to a page, the more important that page is to Google." [051_work-session-27-04-2026.txt]
- Placement of internal links: "it is better to have them at the higher end of the content, not so much the bottom, as well as integrated throughout the content. Google likes to see that more than if it's just like three suggested blogs at the bottom." A mix of in-text links and a suggested-posts block is "the sweet spot." [050_technical-seo-with-elliot-30-04-2026.txt]
- CID link inside blog posts and service pages: link back to your Google Business Profile with the CID link "wherever you can insert the link naturally"; it passes authority and raises profile visibility. [047_technical-seo-with-elliot-08-05-2026.txt]
- David (auto repair, Texas) recipe per post: three internal service links plus one authoritative external link (a manufacturer technical service bulletin); his posts rank number one for things like "Chevy Cruze transmission problems." [044_technical-seo-with-elliot-14-05-2026.txt]
- Local keyword in blog posts: keep the body generic to avoid interfering with location money pages; add a line at the bottom ("if you're looking for website design in West Virginia") linking to the money page. [051_work-session-27-04-2026.txt]
- Strip AI formatting before pasting: copy from Claude into a plain text document first (or use WordPress's paste-as-text clipboard button) so no Claude/div tags land in the HTML; or have ChatGPT output HTML and paste in the code tab. [046_work-session-11-05-2026.txt]
- Post length example: a PressMaster-generated post from the interview answers was "about 600 to 900 words"; Matt's only concern was humanizing ChatGPT output (Claude output needs less cleanup). [046_work-session-11-05-2026.txt]
- WordPress tags and categories: "don't use tags because people use tags in the wrong way. And then it just creates a bunch of like really low content pages. Choose five to 10 categories and each blog post goes into one of those categories." Meta keywords field: ignore, it does nothing. [048_work-session-04-05-2026.txt]
- Tag and category archive pages: set to noindex; "those pages as a whole don't provide any value and they are relatively useless... Those aren't going to be your money pages." A tag page like blog/tag-AI-monetization will not be indexed anyway. [044_technical-seo-with-elliot-14-05-2026.txt]
