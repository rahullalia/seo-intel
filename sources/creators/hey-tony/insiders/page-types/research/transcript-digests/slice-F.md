## 1. Homepage

Structure and section order (Leanne's framework)
- The typical structure of a website is a navigation bar at the top, then a hero section: "the main attraction of your website. It's the first impression people get when they land on your website." The hero must be impactful and "help direct the customer into what they should do next." [055_web-design-for-seo-with-leanne-15-04-2026.txt]
- Hero components: the H1 tag, some sub text that describes the heading a little better, and a call to action button. Include it right at the top "because it gives somebody an action what they should do next without even having to scroll, without even really having to think." [055_web-design-for-seo-with-leanne-15-04-2026.txt]
- Leanne's homepage wireframe ("my framework for designing home pages"): hero with the H1, then trust-building elements (either inside the hero or just below it), then a section introducing who we are ("essentially we're kind of introducing ourselves to our customers... they don't know anything about us"), then the services. Her massage-therapy client example: "why choose us", a short intro, who we work with ("so automatically people are understanding, oh yeah, this is actually a good fit for me"), then the services. She shared the wireframe as a workbook / Drive folder of assets; it "gives you a good outline for how much copy to write." [055_web-design-for-seo-with-leanne-15-04-2026.txt]
- Service-based sites must build trust: introduce yourselves and add "reviews throughout the website." A graphic designer (Katya) objected that shoppers don't care who owns the store; Leanne's answer: for a service, "one of the main things about providing like a graphic design service is people working with you", so put a small blurb about yourself on the homepage and link to the about page ("just have like this little blurb on your homepage and then link to learn more about you"). Services can sit higher if you prefer, but the blurb still belongs on the homepage. [055_web-design-for-seo-with-leanne-15-04-2026.txt]

## 2. Service pages (parent/overview and individual child service pages) and money pages in general

One topic per page, page per service
- "Every single page on your website is about one main topic"; don't chase the same keyword on several pages; you can mention it elsewhere but it shouldn't be the focus. [061_why-doesn-t-it-rank-03-04-2026.txt]
- "The only way to rank is if you have a page about that specific topic." String quartet: a vague "packages and pricing" page should become pages per ensemble (quartet, trio, duo, solo musician) with rates per event type (weddings, corporate); keyword research (string trio, string quartet Los Angeles, live strings) dictates which pages to create. [061_why-doesn-t-it-rank-03-04-2026.txt]
- Bookkeeping site "who we work with" list (HVAC, landscaping, pressure washing, pest control, pool service, junk removal): make a page per industry ("bookkeeping for HVAC companies"); volume will be minimal or zero but intent is high; each page must speak to that industry's specific issues, e.g. mention the vendors an HVAC or electrical business buys from, where those vendors are (local relevancy), and link to them. "You don't have to do this overnight." [061_why-doesn-t-it-rank-03-04-2026.txt]
- Graphic/web designer with no service page: add one and focus it on one service. [055_web-design-for-seo-with-leanne-15-04-2026.txt]
- Holiday decorator: separate pages per season and per service (design, installation/takedown, storage). [061_why-doesn-t-it-rank-03-04-2026.txt]
- Hypnotherapist (Jillian): every page's meta title carrying the same keyword "hypnotherapy" is "a problem... probably going to be flagged as duplicate content"; differentiate with location or sub-topic (hypnotherapy + location, hypnotherapy best practices). Builder SEO scorers that ask for one keyword per page "gamify SEO" and can mislead. [063_why-doesn-t-it-rank-27-03-2026.txt]
- Each money page should be about one specific keyword; keyword variations can be added to the audit sheet; KD 20-30 is achievable but slower. [056_work-session-13-04-2026.txt]

On-page: keyword, H1, title, URL, density
- Find a competitor's focus keyword via their title tag, meta description and H1; or run pageaudit.com without a target keyword and read word density at 3 words. Matt's SEO page ranks #1 in Hamilton without Hamilton in the URL, H1 or H2s; the title/meta carry "SEO in Hamilton". [058_why-doesn-t-it-rank-10-04-2026.txt]
- URL: keep it short, e.g. site.com/services/bookkeeping; the full long keyword in the URL "can look spammy." The long phrase "Bookkeeping services in St. Petersburg, Florida for small business" can be the H1 and title tag but not the URL. Use the exact (primary) keyword plus easier/longer-tail secondary keywords; copy "should sound normal if a person was reading it like a human"; mirror roughly what competitors do. [058_why-doesn-t-it-rank-10-04-2026.txt]
- Title tags on money pages should include the location ("Should title tags have our location on your money pages? Yes."). [058_why-doesn-t-it-rank-10-04-2026.txt]
- Company name at the end of every title tag "is not going to hurt it, but it also might not help"; Matt's all end in "- Hey Tony". [061_why-doesn-t-it-rank-03-04-2026.txt]
- Location in the H1 of a money page is fine even when a tool flags it because local competitors don't do it (Al, SF Bay Area; he lives outside the area he serves). [061_why-doesn-t-it-rank-03-04-2026.txt]
- Bookkeeping homepage/service: the top-ranking competitor uses "bookkeeping" 27 times (mostly brand name), has a terrible title, no meta description, H1 without the keyword; Matt: "use it three or four times and see what happens." [061_why-doesn-t-it-rank-03-04-2026.txt]
- Cat-behavior consultant: the tool's suggested phrase "cat fighting help" is awkward as an H1; turn it into a natural phrase like "stop your cats from fighting"; do keyword research across all the synonyms (cat behaviorist, cat trainer, cat coach...) and pick one or two. [058_why-doesn-t-it-rank-10-04-2026.txt]
- IT company with /managed-it-services URLs while "IT support" is the better keyword: only change a URL if the page isn't ranking, isn't getting traffic, isn't making money and has no backlinks; otherwise switch the keyword in the content and keep the URL. Avoid industry jargon ("help desk"); use what the average business owner types ("my computer is broken", "IT support", "computer services near me"), or niche to "virtual IT support". [054_why-doesn-t-it-rank-17-04-2026.txt]
- Don't change service-page URLs casually: "every time you change it, even if you change like one letter, it's a whole new URL. Google needs to re index it"; /newborn-photography is fine as is. Check three things before changing any slug: is the page ranking, getting traffic, or has backlinks; and update every internal link to the new URL. A member (Lori) who changed her money-page URL during Rank Week lost indexing for weeks; the old URL may have had more "juice". ## 3. Location / city / service-area pages

- Navigation labels are not heading tags. [058_why-doesn-t-it-rank-10-04-2026.txt]

- A Vehicles nav item needs a dropdown or a landing page that actually links the sub-pages (orphans). [061_why-doesn-t-it-rank-03-04-2026.txt]
- Put Blog in the main menu (everyone forgets). [058_why-doesn-t-it-rank-10-04-2026.txt]

- Top announcement bar: use it for an offer (10% coupon, free shipping threshold) and make it static. [060_web-design-for-seo-01-04-2026.txt]
- Phone number and email in the header, so the body doesn't need call/email/WhatsApp buttons. [063_why-doesn-t-it-rank-27-03-2026.txt]

Footer

Sidebars

## 15. General page rules that apply to every page (length, density, images, headings, speed, mobile, readability, AEO/answer-first) when framed as page-building guidance

Keywords, headings, titles
- One main topic/keyword per page; keyword at the start of the H1 and title tag; meta description is a click pitch, never a copy of the title. [061_why-doesn-t-it-rank-03-04-2026.txt]
- The H1 should be the first heading and say exactly what the page is; one H1 per page; correct H1 > H2 > H3 order; heading structure is cleanup work, internal linking comes first after a migration (Jennifer's HTML-to-WordPress move: keep URLs, title and meta identical, then internal links, then fix H1s). [061_why-doesn-t-it-rank-03-04-2026.txt] [059_technical-seo-with-denroi-10-04-2026.txt]
- Pages with no H1 are a big problem ("that tells Google what the page is about"); Bryn found 1,200 pages without H1s. [062_work-session-30-03-2026.txt]
- Titles too long (store name appended) and no emojis in titles. [059_technical-seo-with-denroi-10-04-2026.txt] [061_why-doesn-t-it-rank-03-04-2026.txt]
- Keyword frequency: use the primary keyword a few times (three or four as a starting test), plus natural variations; over-optimization and too-low density are both possible; don't obsess over density tools. [061_why-doesn-t-it-rank-03-04-2026.txt] [058_why-doesn-t-it-rank-10-04-2026.txt] [053_work-session-20-04-2026.txt]
- Keep URLs short; don't change them unless the page has no rankings, traffic or backlinks; update all internal links when you do; 301 old to related new. [058_why-doesn-t-it-rank-10-04-2026.txt] [062_work-session-30-03-2026.txt]
- Use plain-language terms, not industry jargon. [054_why-doesn-t-it-rank-17-04-2026.txt]

Length and content
- Homepage target around 2,000 words for the auto shop (competition at 2,200); "2,000 words max" for an overlong homepage; blogs 500-800 words minimum, 1,500-2,000 to compete; under 300 words gets flagged. [055_web-design-for-seo-with-leanne-15-04-2026.txt] [061_why-doesn-t-it-rank-03-04-2026.txt] [063_why-doesn-t-it-rank-27-03-2026.txt]
- Information gap: "what new information are you providing to users that your competition isn't?" (case studies, opinions, experience). [057_work-session-06-04-2026.txt]
- Answer first; don't make people scroll for the answer. [063_why-doesn-t-it-rank-27-03-2026.txt] [056_work-session-13-04-2026.txt]
- Don't copy competitors too closely. [063_why-doesn-t-it-rank-27-03-2026.txt]
- Schema: "I haven't seen schema markup... really make that much of a difference" for AI visibility; content and blogs matter most. Denroy's practical rule when you do use it: LocalBusiness schema on the homepage, per-city schema on city pages. [062_work-session-30-03-2026.txt] [064_technical-seo-with-denroi-26-03-2026.txt]

Design and readability
- White space: "white space doesn't mean just white. It just means the empty space around objects and assets"; margins, line height, letter spacing, columns (4-column grid); left-align long text, center only short blurbs; align buttons with the text. [055_web-design-for-seo-with-leanne-15-04-2026.txt]
- Alternate text-left/image-right rows and subtle background shifts to break up long pages. [055_web-design-for-seo-with-leanne-15-04-2026.txt] [060_web-design-for-seo-01-04-2026.txt]
- Fonts: sans serif = down to earth, easy to read, good default; serif = luxurious/elevated, match headline font to a serif logo; use Google Fonts (free, loads reliably); cursive/script body fonts are hard to read ("make it as easy as possible for people"); changing one word's color needs a span/custom code. [055_web-design-for-seo-with-leanne-15-04-2026.txt] [060_web-design-for-seo-01-04-2026.txt] [061_why-doesn-t-it-rank-03-04-2026.txt]
- Colors: pick palettes that match brand energy (navy/gold for jewelry, muted for corporate); secondary colors shouldn't be louder than the CTA; ensure contrast passes the black-and-white test. [060_web-design-for-seo-01-04-2026.txt]
- Design from the largest screen down to phone so layouts expand and contract cleanly; Squarespace layouts breaking between desktop/laptop/mobile is a reason to move to WordPress (Elementor or Avada). [060_web-design-for-seo-01-04-2026.txt]
- Fewer, clearer CTAs; one primary button; secondary as outline. [060_web-design-for-seo-01-04-2026.txt] [063_why-doesn-t-it-rank-27-03-2026.txt]
- Reduce animation and moving elements. [060_web-design-for-seo-01-04-2026.txt]
- Real photography over stock; hero 1440 x 740. [055_web-design-for-seo-with-leanne-15-04-2026.txt]

Speed and mobile
- Speed is a tiebreaker, not everything: "if yours is slow and your competitors are fast and everything else is equal, they... would outrank you. But I've seen slow websites outrank fast ones." Squarespace: only images can be optimized (mobile 39 / desktop 75 example). [058_why-doesn-t-it-rank-10-04-2026.txt]
- Speed priorities: run PageSpeed Insights first, CDN, WebP images (upload as WebP rather than relying on a plugin), compression, caching (WP Rocket or SiteGround optimizer), delay JavaScript and test for breakage, current PHP, good hosting (SiteGround recommended); videos external; remove carousels/galleries on Wix; robots.txt/htaccess blocks (Python user agent) can block audit bots. [064_technical-seo-with-denroi-26-03-2026.txt] [060_web-design-for-seo-01-04-2026.txt] [055_web-design-for-seo-with-leanne-15-04-2026.txt]
- Mobile: clickable phone/email, bottom-anchored phone, avoid long text before content on mobile, mobile tap targets and font size are in the rank report card. [054_why-doesn-t-it-rank-17-04-2026.txt] [055_web-design-for-seo-with-leanne-15-04-2026.txt] [063_why-doesn-t-it-rank-27-03-2026.txt]

Indexing and linking
- Every new or updated page: request indexing in GSC; check with site:domain/url; index status via URL inspection. [061_why-doesn-t-it-rank-03-04-2026.txt] [064_technical-seo-with-denroi-26-03-2026.txt]
- Pages discovered but not indexed usually need internal links (and blogs linking to them); link every rank-worthy page from a findable page. [058_why-doesn-t-it-rank-10-04-2026.txt] [062_work-session-30-03-2026.txt]
- Internal "link to existing page" beats pasted URLs. [053_work-session-20-04-2026.txt]
- Avoid tag pages and thin auto-generated pages; avoid letting users publish unmoderated posts; crawl budget is finite. [059_technical-seo-with-denroi-10-04-2026.txt] [061_why-doesn-t-it-rank-03-04-2026.txt]
- Platform: WordPress recommended for flexibility; Wix/Squarespace limit code and layout control; Shopify needs theme edits for H1/title issues. [064_technical-seo-with-denroi-26-03-2026.txt]
- Don't aim for 100% audit scores: "SEO is not about getting a hundred percent perfect scores everywhere. Sometimes you need that D to help you rank." [063_why-doesn-t-it-rank-27-03-2026.txt]
- Don't check rankings daily; compare 28-day windows. [063_why-doesn-t-it-rank-27-03-2026.txt]
- When a site was just migrated or launched, give Google time; request re-index; spikes in non-indexed pages settle in a week or two. [059_technical-seo-with-denroi-10-04-2026.txt] [064_technical-seo-with-denroi-26-03-2026.txt]
