## 2023-04-11 | Programmatic pages are a product built from a template and a live data feed

- Source: Eli Schwartz, "How the Fxck SEO Podcast: Product-Led SEO and SurveyMonkey's $200M Annual Organic Growth Strategy" (Ben Goodey), 2023-04-11 (no page date), https://www.spicymargarita.co/archive/surveymonkey-seo-case-study
- Quote: "Ideally, there's a programmatic solution to that. Just look at TripAdvisor, Zillow, NerdWallet, stock price, and weather widgets. At its heart, it's a product. Programmatic is a tactic to create that product at scale."
- Means: Build a programmatic page set as a product fed by a database or API, not as a batch of written articles. Schwartz built this approach at SurveyMonkey over seven years into a reported two hundred million dollars a year in organic revenue.
- Factor: programmatic-templates-unique-data | Evidence: D | Lever: ranking | Scope: organic | Stage: build

## 2023-03-13 | Numeric values inside a programmatic template refresh from the API, not a rewrite
