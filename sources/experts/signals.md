## 2026-10-12 | Reddit's share of ChatGPT citations fell from about 1 answer in 5 to about 1 in 43
- Source: Ahrefs, Louise Linehan, "How ChatGPT Does Query Fan-Out (15 Million Queries Studied)", 2026-10-12, https://ahrefs.com/blog/query-fan-out-chatgpt/
- Quote: "In March, about 1 in every 5 ChatGPT answers cited Reddit, but by late September it was about 1 in 43."
- Means: The figure comes from Brand Radar's US ChatGPT citations, 2.4 to 3.8 million answers a month from March to September 2026, and Ahrefs reports Wikipedia falling from 1 in 7 answers to 1 in 35 and YouTube from about 1 in 370 to about 1 in 500 over the same period. The reddit-mentions row rests on two grade C HeyTony tests both dated 2026-07, and this grade B measurement puts the citation share those tests worked against roughly eight times lower over seven months.
- Factor: reddit-mentions | Evidence: B | Lever: ranking | Scope: ai | Stage: build

## 2026-09-24 | A 100 location franchise gained 20 to 30 percent in rankings while calls fell 40 percent
- Source: Near Media, Greg Sterling and Mike Blumenthal with Andrew Shotland and Mark Kabana, "EP 275 - Rankings Up, Calls Down: What AI Visibility Is Really Worth to Local Businesses (Part 2)", 2026-09-24, https://www.nearmedia.co/ep-275-rankings-up-calls-down-what-ai-visibility-is-really-worth-to-local-businesses-part-2/
- Quote: "Their rankings are up like 20 to 30 percent year over year. So, hooray for us. Their clicks and calls are down 40%, right?"
- Means: Shotland said he cannot separate AI Overviews, more ads and falling demand as the cause, and Mike Blumenthal's reading on the same call was that ads took the clicks, citing a storage query whose first screen ran 43 percent ads. A rank report alone no longer tells a local client whether the work produced calls, so report call volume next to it.
- Factor: position-one-ctr | Evidence: C | Lever: conversion | Scope: local organic | Stage: audit

## 2025-12-15 | The informational share of AI Overview queries fell from 91.3 to 57.1 percent in ten months
- Source: Semrush, "Semrush AI Overviews Study", 2025-12-15, https://www.semrush.com/blog/semrush-ai-overviews-study/
- Quote: "In January, 91.3% of queries that trigger an AI Overview were informational. By October, that share was down to 57.1%, and the share of commercial and transactional AIOs rose."
- Means: Semrush analyzed more than 10 million keywords through 2025. Among keywords carrying an AI Overview, commercial intent rose from 8.15 to 18.57 percent and transactional intent from 1.98 to 13.94 percent since October 2024. A purchase-intent page is less exposed to an AI Overview than an article, but it is not exempt, and the gap narrowed through 2025.
- Factor: position-one-ctr | Evidence: B | Lever: conversion | Scope: organic ai | Stage: both

## 2025-04-17 | An AI Overview on the results page went with a 34.5 percent lower click rate for the top result
- Source: Ahrefs, Ryan Law, "AI Overviews Reduce Clicks by 34.5%", 2025-04-17, https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overviews-reduce-clicks/
- Quote: "We analyzed 300,000 keywords and found that the presence of an AI Overview in the search results correlated with a 34.5% lower average clickthrough rate (CTR) for the top-ranking page, compared to similar informational keywords without an AI Overview."
- Means: Ahrefs compared position one click rates in March 2024 and March 2025 across 150,000 keywords with an AI Overview and 150,000 informational keywords without one. The same page states that 99.2 percent of the keywords triggering an AI Overview were informational at the time, so the loss fell on articles. Edward Sturm's Compact Keywords sales page cites this figure as its case against blog SEO.
- Factor: position-one-ctr | Evidence: B | Lever: conversion | Scope: organic ai | Stage: both
