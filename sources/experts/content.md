## 2026-09-14 | An answer inside the first 100 words beat every other on-page edit in a GEO test
- Source: Search Engine Land, Zeeshan Yaseen, "Two GEO experiments challenge conventional AI visibility advice", 2026-09-14, https://searchengineland.com/geo-experiments-challenge-conventional-ai-visibility-advice-488342
- Quote: "In our tests, pages performed better when the answer appeared within the first 100 words. A key takeaway block near the top of the page produced a larger improvement than any other on-page change we made."
- Means: Yaseen logged 775 citation events across two experiments, each tracking 15 commercial-intent keywords checked by hand, and ranks the top-of-page takeaway block above every other on-page change he made. Open the page with the answer in the first 100 words and keep the context below it.
- Factor: answer-first-structure | Evidence: C | Lever: ranking | Scope: ai organic | Stage: both

## 2026-09-14 | FAQ answers open by default outperformed the same answers inside expandable sections
- Source: Search Engine Land, Zeeshan Yaseen, "Two GEO experiments challenge conventional AI visibility advice", 2026-09-14, https://searchengineland.com/geo-experiments-challenge-conventional-ai-visibility-advice-488342
- Quote: "FAQ content performed better when it was visible by default rather than hidden behind expandable sections."
- Means: This is a practitioner test pointing the same way as Cyrus Shepard's Content Visibility factor of 2026-05-07, which scored 54 published sources rather than running a test. Yaseen reports the finding from the first of his two experiments, which tracked 15 commercial-intent keywords across four platforms. Leave FAQ answers expanded in the markup on any page meant to be cited by an assistant.
- Factor: hidden-accordion-content | Evidence: C | Lever: ranking | Scope: ai organic | Stage: both

## 2026-09-09 | Original research and first-party data topped the content quality factors in a 131 person survey
- Source: Zyppy, Cyrus Shepard and Dawn Shepard, "Google Ranking Factors Expert Survey 2026", 2026-09-09, https://signal.zyppy.com/p/google-ranking-factors-expert-survey
- Quote: "“Original Research” and “First-Party Data” top the list, reflecting the importance of producing unique, non-commodity content in the age of AI."
- Means: 131 respondents scored 103 factors across 13,665 data points, and 47.6 percent named content quality a top three factor, third behind relevance at 57.1 percent and backlinks at 54.8 percent. A page carrying numbers the agency measured itself scores higher on this list than one restating published material.
- Factor: information-gain | Evidence: B | Lever: ranking | Scope: organic | Stage: both

## 2026-09-09 | Respondents marked AI content down for scale without added value, not for being AI
- Source: Zyppy, Cyrus Shepard and Dawn Shepard, "Google Ranking Factors Expert Survey 2026", 2026-09-09, https://signal.zyppy.com/p/google-ranking-factors-expert-survey
- Quote: "Many saw AI-generated content negatively, but only when it is scaled with little added value."
- Means: The 131 respondents separated production method from output quality, which is what the Ahrefs study of 600,000 pages dated 2025-07-08 already measured. The penalty respondents described attaches to thin pages published at volume, not to the tool that wrote them.
- Factor: ai-generated-content | Evidence: B | Lever: ranking | Scope: organic | Stage: both

## 2026-05-07 | Text locked behind a click, like a closed accordion or tab, counts as hidden
- Source: Zyppy Signal, Cyrus Shepard, "AI Citation Ranking Factors Analysis", 2026-05-07, https://signal.zyppy.com/p/ai-citation-ranking-factors
- Quote: "Modern web pages can contain a lot of text that's not immediately visible, at least not without a lot of JavaScript or by requiring users to click on divs and tabs."
- Means: Shepard's Content Visibility factor, 7.6 of 9.5 across 54 sources, names clicking into a div or tab as a way content becomes invisible to an AI crawler. Move the direct answer sentence outside a collapsed accordion even when supporting detail stays inside one.
- Factor: hidden-accordion-content | Evidence: B | Lever: ranking | Scope: ai | Stage: both

## 2025-12-15 | Grade 6 to 8 reading level holds up in AI Mode citation counts
- Source: SE Ranking, "How to Optimize for AI Mode: Google Visibility Matters 3x More Than Content", 2025-12-15, https://seranking.com/blog/how-to-optimize-for-ai-mode/
- Quote: "accessible yet informative text (Grade 6-8) maintains around 4.6"

## 2025-07-08 | A 600,000 page Ahrefs study found no link between AI content and rankings
- Source: Ahrefs, "AI-Generated Content Does Not Hurt Your Google Rankings (600,000 Pages Analyzed)", 2025-07-07, https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-generated-content-does-not-hurt-your-google-rankings/
- Source: Search Engine Journal, "Ahrefs Study Finds No Evidence Google Penalizes AI Content", 2025-07-08, https://www.searchenginejournal.com/ahrefs-study-finds-no-evidence-google-penalizes-ai-content/550656/
- Quote: "There is no clear relationship between how much AI-generated content a page has and how highly it ranks on Google."
- Means: Ahrefs checked 600,000 webpages and found no correlation between the amount of AI generated content on a page and its Google ranking. Stop auditing a client's pages for how the text was produced and audit them for whether the page answers the query.
- Factor: ai-generated-content | Evidence: B | Lever: ranking | Scope: organic | Stage: both

## 2024-06-28 | Citing sources and adding statistics raised visibility in generative engine answers by 30 to 40 percent

- Quote: "Cite Sources, Quotation Addition, and Statistics Addition, achieved a relative improvement of 30-40%"
