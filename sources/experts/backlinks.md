## 2023-12-01 | Pages with zero referring domains almost never reach 1,000 organic visits a month
- Source: Ahrefs, "96.55% of Content Gets No Traffic From Google. Here's How to Be in the Other 3.45% [New Research for 2023]", 2023-12-01, https://ahrefs.com/blog/search-traffic-study/
- Quote: "Our index contains ~20 million pages with no referring domains, yet only 2,997 of them get more than 1K search visits per month. That's roughly 1 in every 6,671 pages with no backlinks."
- Means: Ahrefs studied its Content Explorer database of about 14 billion pages and counted referring domains per page, so a page built for a competitive query needs links from other domains before traffic is realistic. Pages with no referring domains still get traffic on low difficulty queries, which is the case for targeting uncompetitive topics first.
- Factor: referring-domains | Evidence: B | Lever: ranking | Scope: organic | Stage: both

## 2018-09-18 | Number one pages add new referring domains at 5 to 14.5 percent per month
- Source: Ahrefs, "How Many New Backlinks Do Top-ranking Pages Get Over Time [New Data by Ahrefs]", 2018-09-18, https://ahrefs.com/blog/backlink-growth-study/
- Quote: "It turns out that most of the #1 ranking pages tend to get followed backlinks (from new referring domains) at a pace of between +5% and +14.5% per month."
- Means: Ahrefs pulled the top 20 ranking pages for 10,000 non-branded keywords in the 2K to 5K monthly volume range, 200,000 pages in total, and measured followed referring domains acquired in the previous three months. Matching a competitor's current referring domain count is not enough, because that count keeps growing while the link building runs.
- Factor: referring-domains | Evidence: B | Lever: ranking | Scope: organic | Stage: both
